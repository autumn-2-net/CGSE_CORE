Captured candidate result handoff
================================

A work/time/memory limit may arrive after an inner canonical proof has finished
but before the outer captured catalog publishes it. CapturedCatalog retains an
INFEASIBLE result only when relevant capture coverage is complete and the active
solver is the canonical view. Prefix and allocation/flow support results cannot
prove the full request impossible. Ordinary missing previews are not proofs.

A verified positive witness remains valid even for a restricted view. Handoff
does not claim its optimality. Cancellation before or during child harvesting
remains cancellation, including when the shared budget has already timed out.
Callers continue to own close(); repeated cleanup is safe.

CapturedHandoffTest drives the actual production continuations to their proof
or verified-witness publication boundary. Reflection only observes internal
state; no proof flags/results are injected. An independent sibling thread then
exhausts shared work, refuses a memory reservation, or advances an injected clock.
It checks complete and incomplete coverage, 256-prefix alternatives with a real
omitted witness, allocation support and unstartable flow proposals, unproved
missing previews, stock/new-producer changes, candidate order, cancellation, and
zero remaining memory reservations after cleanup.
