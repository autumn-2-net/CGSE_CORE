Root proof aggregation and numeric continuations (2026-10-09)
============================================================

Root conclusions
----------------
The stein27_inf finite recipe replay did not merely need a larger search budget.
CountCardinalitySearch exhausted its full root domain after a retained structural
search. IntegerCountBranch learned the empty conflict, but did not identify this
as a completed root proof. An earlier unresolved child kept IntegerCountSearch's
unresolved flag set, so the final answer was UNKNOWN despite that contradiction.

Completed structural and LP root contradictions now use the same root-proof
handoff as equivalent-view proofs. The coordinator checks the original model,
DEAD state and empty branch assumptions before accepting it. A guarded child,
local heuristic failure or exhausted allowance does not become a root proof.
The existing earlier scan of completed workers still protects a finished proof
from optional cache publication encountering the shared budget limit.

Arithmetic ownership
--------------------
CountAffineLattice checkpoints complete unimodular column transforms, individual
Gram-Schmidt projections, complete LLL size reductions and swaps. Intermediate
LLL reduction after coefficient growth now also retains its pivot and return
phase. A local handoff cannot resume a partly updated matrix. Nearest-plane
trials and low-dimensional interval checks finish their bounded atomic step.

CountKernelSearch prepares lazily. It retains orthogonal/dual coefficients,
coordinate bounds, projected rows, the inner JUMP search and restoration and
original-row verification cursors. A resumed projection never starts from an
empty matrix. A candidate is published only after every original bound and row
has been checked, including signed and larger-than-long coordinates.

CountDiophantine retains its affine child or low-dimensional face enumerator and
charges nested work to its local clock. Its paused original-coordinate search
can transfer to CountViewSearch under exactly matching rows and bounds. The
conditional LCG wrapper alternates with the retained affine arm instead of
discarding a paid factorization or monopolizing the proving frontier.

Continuation is opt-in. Precision refusal, admission failure and natural trial
face limits remain terminal candidate failures. None proves infeasibility.
The shared request budget/cancellation is checked inside arithmetic; only local
time slices yield at coherent checkpoints. A bounded atomic step may cross its
local quota, but does not refund, reset or extend the request allowance. Deadline
extension is saturating. Paused polling allocates and charges nothing. The
48-coordinate lattice admission and existing precision limits are unchanged.

Independent remaining-case truth
-------------------------------
tests/oracles/verify_count_models.py uses optional test-only CP-SAT, SCIP and Z3.
No native solver is a production dependency. Witnesses are independently checked
with Python integers against the original model. Unbounded variables are not
clipped; CP-SAT and inexact double representations explicitly decline them.

The eight previously UNKNOWN finite records are two SAT lseu-1120 normalized
views and six UNSAT lseu-1119 views. CP-SAT and SCIP agree on all eight; Z3 reaches
its 15-second allowance on these. These are oracle cross-checks, not exported
CGSE proof certificates. Historical source truth labels remain unchanged.

The six unbounded conversion exclusions have four exact-checked SAT witnesses,
one Z3 UNSAT result and one still UNKNOWN. The existing raw-view CGSE entry
handles them without conversion: two conclude, four remain unresolved. They are
not silently removed from the finite recipe embedding's exclusion count.

Versions, original input hashes and integer witnesses are preserved in
tests/provenance/numeric-oracles-20261009.json. Optional replay:
  python tests/oracles/verify_count_models.py --seconds 15

Validation scope
----------------
NumericContinuationTest compares 80 paired interrupted/uninterrupted numeric
runs, including signed/80-bit offsets; all return the same valid witness. It
exercises 288 local pauses, projected-kernel interruption, scope mismatch,
cancelled/exhausted/overflow-sized resume requests, memory refusal, idempotent
disposal and 32 numerical tasks on four workers with base/expanded budgets.
CountKernelTest continues checking 480 finite boxes against exhaustive truth.

The stable suite, 239 curated and 800 expanded weighted graph fixtures (three
orders each), full captured catalog replay and four-worker budget campaigns are
recorded in tests/provenance/numeric-continuation-20261009.json. Captures are
offline replay, not a fresh live Minecraft run. No wall-time speedup, measured
heap/RSS or general SCIP/CP-SAT equivalence is asserted by logical-work counts.

22 original-order count limits remain: after the independent truth checks,
15 are known SAT and seven known UNSAT. The next gains require stronger bounded
search for the larger sparse/wide equalities, subset sums and lseu bound family;
these cases were not relabeled as solved by improving continuation infrastructure.
