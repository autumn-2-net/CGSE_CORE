Exact price propagation in the retained packing LP (0.1.6)

The deferred LP candidate arm already reconstructed an integer upper bound
from numerical prices, but used it only to reject the entire current box.
It now also fixes coordinates whose opposite value cannot reach the demand.
The same certificate first uses checked primitive arithmetic, retrying the
whole calculation with BigInteger if any intermediate value overflows.

Certificate and scope
---------------------
For capacity rows A*x <= b, nonnegative integer prices y, profit p and scale
q=65536, let r=q*p-y*A. Within the node's fixed Boolean box, an upper bound is

    U = y*b + sum(fixed-one r[j]) + sum(free max(0,r[j])).

The order requires q*p*x >= q*target. A negative gap U-q*target rejects this
box. For a free coordinate, setting a positive r[j] to zero loses r[j];
setting a negative r[j] to one loses -r[j]. A loss strictly greater than the
gap forces the opposite value. Equality permits the value and must not prune
it. All such fixings are individually necessary in the same original box,
so they can be applied together. LP optimality and floating point tolerances
are not premises: arbitrary nonnegative price proposals retain this property.

This is the Boolean-box counterpart of exact reduced-cost reasoning, rather
than a new objective cutoff for the general CountReducedCost proof service.
The current face's demand and inherited fixed exterior are mandatory premises.
No fixings or unsuccessful numerical search are exported to another face,
view, order, or the whole-model infeasibility channel.

The fixed mask lives in the existing depth frame. Fixing a nonbasic variable
updates its basic coordinates to preserve capacity equations; the next step
reoptimizes the retained basis before rounding or branching. Child frames
inherit their parent, and reused sibling buffers reset to that parent.
The original retained enumeration, PB sample, deferred grant and request cap
are unchanged. Every candidate still passes all projected rows, restoration
to original coordinates and primitive execution/reservation/target checks.

Arithmetic and storage
----------------------
The common path keeps prices and reduced coefficients in reusable long
arrays. A precomputed absolute objective magnitude and row activities give
a cheap envelope for products, accumulated bounds, differences and the gap.
Oversized certificates go directly to BigInteger; remaining primitive
operations also use checked arithmetic. Overflow restarts the identical
certificate before any domain change. It never saturates a bound or rounds
a demand. Fixing a nonbasic coordinate already at the required value avoids
an unchanged basis update, using exact equality rather than a tolerance.
The additional arrays are covered by an 8KiB increase in the optional LP
workspace reservation. The arrays belong to this request; there is no new
cross-order cache, cache budget or runtime solver dependency.

The first candidate tried narrow arithmetic and caught overflow on every
large-number evaluation. A local kernel benchmark exposed the repeated
exception cost. The absolute-envelope precheck removes that path before the
final comparison. Both versions, and the independent arithmetic-only and
propagation-only experiments, remain archived rather than overwriting evidence.

Validation
----------
At the unchanged 40M search cap, 256MiB reservation limit and no wall timeout,
the final library preserves all 147 positives in the complete 296-input
packing comparison against a95d0835; 149 remain unresolved. This increment
recovers no additional canonical case. The inherited backlog stays 155:
149 packing, four sparse equality and two vector allocation cases.

mknapcb3_01_bks falls from 38,862,924 to 38,412,277 reported work (-1.160%).
Its retained LP uses 1707 nodes and 4835 pivots instead of 1863 and 6612;
2317 exact implications are applied. mknapcb6_09_bks saves 87 work, while
the other 145 old positives keep identical work. Old-positive aggregate work
falls 0.0403%, and all 147 reserved peaks stay unchanged. This is a targeted
improvement, not an across-the-board planner speedup.

Forty-eight fresh order/scheduling requests, compared with completed
a95d0835 results at identical frozen inputs and budgets, retain all 46
positives. Order97 of mknapcb3_01_bks remains unresolved in both serial and
fixed-four-worker execution. The other seven orders pass in both modes,
at no more than 38,446,381 reported work. Their 14 existing mandatory guards
are tightened from 39M to 38.6M; the suite inventory stays 747 requests.
Other old ordered positives add at most 1247 work and retain their guards,
so the canonical improvement is not a claim that every permutation is cheaper.
The final canonical word also passes independent Python integer replay of
all 148 primitive firings.

The maintained LP test exhaustively checks 192 original Boolean domains,
preserving all 128 SAT witnesses. Across 1536 arbitrary-price fixed boxes,
306 rejected boxes and 1062 simultaneous fixings are checked against every
original assignment. Boundaries include strict equality, products and sums
outside long, an overflowing gap, Long.MIN_VALUE activity, values beyond
double's exact range, original capacity equations after fixing, sibling
restoration, mixed rows, cancellation, memory refusal and worker handoff.

Twenty-five related maintained programs and eight final-library checks pass.
Ten injected faults are caught by the final tests. The first two mutation
runs were partial: they exposed missing coverage of a wrapping capacity
product with tiny profit and an omitted basic-coordinate update. Both
independent invariant checks are now maintained; those runs remain archived
as partial, not reported as successful campaigns.

The isolated certificate benchmark uses identical prices and boxes, ABBA
library order, three warmup rounds and six measured rounds per process.
For 4096 narrow evaluations, median thread allocation falls from 185,956,864
to 98,304 bytes; the latter is reflection-call overhead in the driver.
The wide control falls from 214,497,600 to 174,487,872 bytes. Wide reported
work rises from 839,680 to 1,003,520 because fixing scans are additional work.
The final wide thread-CPU median returns to the baseline's 31.25ms, versus
140.625ms in the discarded exception-heavy variant. Windows thread-CPU
resolution is too coarse to report a narrow-path speed ratio. These kernel
measurements do not measure whole-planner or host performance.

Offline assemble, all 1478 baseline ABI entries, source-JAR correspondence
and the combined Forge/AE-dump artifact checks pass at version 0.1.6.
The complete historical corpus and all 747 execution boundaries were not
rerun. No new native-solver or live-host run is claimed.
