Algorithm capacity versus dispatch
----------------------------------
Direct original-coordinate replays give an existing tool the same entire 20M
request allowance, with 256 MiB reservations and no wall deadline. The retained
LP/LCG arm proves all seven lseu cases; retained affine search finds witnesses
for both 96-variable equalities. The baseline integrated count stage solves
none of these nine. Direct and embedded models have different representations,
so that comparison alone would not establish a scheduling bug.

Controlled in-pipeline experiments confirm it: extending the SAME LP frontier
before root/child fan-out closes all seven negatives, and extending the SAME
adopted numeric frontier closes the two positives. Neither needs new inference,
extra threads, a larger request limit or weaker plan verification. Every
positive is checked against the original rows, domains and primitive execution.

The other four equality cases have 144 or 257 variables. The current affine
tool's ambient/kernel admission limits exclude those raw models. A local-only
experiment widened them from 128/48 to 288/144; the four original residuals
still did not solve at 20M. Removing a size guard alone is not a demonstrated
fix, and these cases do not justify claiming a mathematical impossibility.
This experiment also included two additional same-family records; they are
not counted as newly solved original residuals.

The 48-variable, 48-bit subset sum remains UNKNOWN when its existing four-list
join gets the whole 20M allowance. At an explicit 80M diagnostic cap it returns
an independently checked witness after 76,968,526 work. Thus the tool exists,
but its measured computation does not yet fit the 20M comparison budget. This is a
cost/admission gap, not evidence that all remaining failures are dispatch-only
or that adding one particular new algorithm is necessarily sufficient.

Implemented policy
------------------
CountLpSearch preserves its bounded first sample. Source faces, packing,
decomposition and matching keep their first opportunities to find cheap plans.
At their continuation into Boolean search, an undecided retained LP with real
certified-cut progress can receive more consecutive samples. Completion,
candidate rejection or local cutoff returns to that same Boolean entrance;
it does not replay the scouts or prematurely disperse this frontier into the
root/child queue. Ordinary later root resumes can also use this opportunity.

Only distinct, verified LP consequences count as progress, including after the
exported-row buffer is full. A sample remains at most 262,144 work plus the
existing atomic-operation boundary. Four completed samples without a new cut
end the preference. Preferential LP work has a fixed half-of-entrance-allowance
ceiling. Parent resumes never renew it. Sharing/restoration and atomic work
remain charged to the same request, so this is not a hard reservation of half
the global account against every other concurrent operation.

CountViewSearch pays every eligible arm's mandatory first visit before granting
preference to an adopted Diophantine frontier. The evidence must be two actual
completed exact rank reductions: duplicate equations, fixed coordinates and
trial inequality faces cannot manufacture it. Its ceiling is established once
at seven eighths of the then-remaining allowance; 262,144 charged work without
new numeric progress returns selection to the ordinary policy. The policy can
later select this retained frontier normally. Proof-mode eligibility still
excludes candidate-only numeric algorithms. No cutoff or scheduling observation
is exported as a proof or removed search region.

These fractions and stagnation windows are bounded heuristic choices, not
completeness guarantees. Selection is based on paid, retained progress rather
than model names or a lookup of known verdicts. The general work/time/memory
limits, compiler budget, fallback role, graph semantics and host adapter stay
unchanged. This is an independently implemented scheduling change, not an
import of a general-purpose solver.

Reference topics were deterministic-work allocation in CP-SAT's subsolver
scheduler and observed-outcome scheduling of MIP heuristics:
https://github.com/google/or-tools/blob/stable/ortools/sat/subsolver.cc
https://arxiv.org/abs/2304.03755
The important distinction here is between productive retained work, a cheap
complementary first visit, and completion/proof; turn counts or the existence
of a conflict alone cannot identify those stages.

Rejected policies and regression protection
------------------------------------------
An unconditional early LP extension gained the same 27 ordering conclusions
in the count-only corpus but lost graph conclusions. Capping it at half instead
of three quarters restored those conclusions, yet increased total work on the
717 curated graph checks by 51.8%. Delaying only until source-face attempts
still displaced cheap matching witnesses. Deferring all continuation until
late root resumes preserved graph behavior but lost the seven lseu gains.
None of these intermediate policies is the final implementation.

CountProgressSchedulingTest checks real LP/lattice frontiers, independently
enumerated rank fixtures, fixed/trial/duplicate rows, stagnant observations,
nonrenewable grants, mandatory visits, proof eligibility, cancellation, huge
integer work caps and cleanup. Controlled accounting observations in that test
never alter a proof or domain. RootPortfolioTest separately bounds the paid
work of six existing cheap matching witnesses. Deliberately incorrect rank,
ceiling and early-LP variants must fail these checks.

The 27 newly conclusive full-corpus orderings are pinned at 20M in
tests/regression/count-boundaries.json. The three previous 80M lattice
boundaries remain explicitly separate; no case silently gets a larger cap.
Direct capacity diagnosis is now reproducible with the test-only entries
lp-retained, pb-retained, affine-retained and mitm-retained. For example:

python tests/run.py --java-home <jdk17> --suite models --model-engine lp-retained --case lseu-objective-1119 --permutations 1 --milliseconds 0 --work 20000000 --model-trace --keep-going
python tests/run.py --java-home <jdk17> --suite models --model-engine mitm-retained --case ff19ef4b291940ad997c1dc17e0c4a304210e1b5e8251e0361586aa8f0e2c26c --permutations 1 --milliseconds 0 --work 80000000 --model-trace

Local admission refusals remain UNKNOWN, not UNSUPPORTED conversions or UNSAT.
The direct entries do not call GraphPlanner; main-counts is the integrated
integer stage with graph execution checks, also not the whole planner.
