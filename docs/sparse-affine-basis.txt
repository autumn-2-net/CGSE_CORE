Sparse affine updates and retained support indices
=================================================

Scope
-----
CountAffineLattice now retains nonzero-coordinate words alongside each integer
basis vector. Bezout pairs update the existing two arrays over their support
union; size reduction visits the source support; nearest-plane projection
visits only nonzero basis coordinates. All operations keep ascending coordinate
order, exact integer/rational arithmetic and the existing precision limits.
The old values of both coordinates are read before either array is overwritten.
Cancellation clears bits. Every pivot, removal, reset and deep insertion keeps
the support and vector lists aligned. No candidate admission or request cap is
increased, and no negative proof is produced by this strategy.

Every visited support word and coordinate is charged. The pre-existing Gram
prefix-swap loop now also pays for its visits. The index adds an explicitly
reserved 48 + 8*ceil(ambient/64) bytes per initial basis row (at most 8192 bytes
under the unchanged 128-variable admission limit). Precision refusal remains
terminal; it cannot resume a partially modified pair or publish a candidate.
Point updates retain their full-coordinate precision checks.

Correctness evidence against 2552fcd9
------------------------------------
79 standard programs pass, followed by the strengthened CountSparseAffineTest.
81 paired whole/sliced/handoff models include signed rows, 90-bit offsets and
63/64/65/127/128 coordinate boundaries. The 254155 dense-state audits check
support bits, array aliasing and cancellation. Completed intersections and
subsequent reductions independently check the original equation/kernel
invariants, not only the final witness. An isolated arithmetic check pins word
and coordinate charging. Memory refusal and cancellation release reservations.

Five local negative controls are rejected: omit support swaps, retain cancelled
bits, ignore the second support word, use an overwritten Bezout coordinate, and
omit support-word charging. The second-word mutant can still return a feasible
corner, which is why the tests also verify the entire retained integer kernel.

Isolated throughput
-------------------
compare_throughput.py --baseline 2552fcd9 --retained-affine --allow-work-change
  --engines lattice-sparse,lattice-sparse-offset,lattice,lattice-dense,lattice-offset
  --cases 12 --forks 4 --warmup 3 --samples 5

BC/CB order alternates between fresh JVMs with no concurrent CGSE regression
jobs. Each case retains the same 4M cap. Every returned witness is independently
validated outside the timer; full witnesses must match across revisions and
charged work must be deterministic within each revision. All 60 distinct
generated models produce witnesses in both revisions.

These short batches do not establish a uniform elapsed-time improvement. The
larger dense control is 3.09% slower at the measured median despite doing less
charged work. A targeted follow-up uses the same 12 dense inputs, six BC/CB
forks, ten warmups and twenty samples: 34488400 -> 33526025 ns, or 2.79% faster.
Thus the small elapsed differences are warmup-sensitive, not evidence of a
stable 3% regression or a large universal speedup. Both campaigns are retained.
Allocation is cumulative thread allocation, not peak heap/RSS. The JSON
provenance records per-fork medians and the exact source fingerprints.

Remaining limits
----------------
The four original-order sparse-equality hard cases remain unresolved at 20M.
Local wider affine, delayed-reduction, coefficient-balancing and simultaneous
box-embedding probes did not solve them at that cap and are not admitted into
production. Intermediate coefficient growth and expensive basis reduction
still block this route. This increment does not close wider numeric admission,
the remaining H/J benefit/residency work, or fresh server verification.
