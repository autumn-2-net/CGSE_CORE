Retained box enumeration for vector allocations
==============================================

Problem and scope
-----------------
The maintained manual_small_n40_c3_d3_a1_s43 input finds a checked first-bin
allocation, then stalls in the 26-coordinate kernel of the remaining bin.
Residual finite matching declines that domain. Repeating ordinary integer
kernel moves exhausts the unchanged 40M search cap. This is a count-search
failure, not a durability-model or executable-order regression.

CountVectorPartition enables one complementary enumeration opportunity on
residual bins and first bins with at most 32 capacity groups. Larger first
bins and cheap residual matching retain their previous route. The bound on
first bins limits the cost of a broad sphere tree before any allocation has
shrunk the quantity box. CountAffineLattice admits the scout after its ordinary kernel
has spent 262,144 work; the scout has at most 262,144 additional work, charged
to the same original request and parent local cap. Cooperative atomic steps
can cross that local threshold. Grants, worker handoffs and later face trials
do not replenish the allowance. The ordinary kernel remains owned and resumes
if enumeration declines, runs out of its opportunity or returns no candidate.

The reduced integer basis and current Gram factors are borrowed from the
existing affine search. No second elimination or LLL pass is built. The scout
translates its center around the earlier nearest-plane point, computes its
projection with exact rational arithmetic, then enumerates nearby integer
coordinates using numerical hints. It visits integers nearest the conditional
center first and bounds distance in the orthogonal basis.

A sphere alone contains many vectors outside the original quantity box.
For each partial coordinate assignment, the remaining squared radius times
the coordinate's projection into the remaining orthogonal span bounds its
possible squared movement. If this cannot reach a quantity interval, that
private branch is skipped before reconstructing every leaf. The fixed
component outside the lattice span is included in this calculation.
The sum of squared distances from the fixed vector to all quantity intervals
must also fit within the remaining radius. This joint distance check skips
branches that pass each individual-coordinate test but cannot meet them
together. It shares the existing coordinate loop and adds no workspace.

Correctness and resource boundary
---------------------------------
Numerical pruning is heuristic, not a certified domain fact. Every proposed
vector is reconstructed with BigInteger and checked against every original
integer bound and row before publication. A narrow sphere, rounding error,
nonfinite hint, coordinate-range guard, precision refusal or empty enumeration
cannot export infeasibility, tighten another solver's domains or reject all
other first-bin allocations. Original recipe-prefix/target validation remains
the final authority for a crafting plan.

The scout reserves its arrays before allocation, counts preparation and search
against its owner, and retains projection and tree state across handoffs.
Cancellation, quota exhaustion and close release its workspace. Reset closes
it before changing the borrowed basis. After a verified candidate, the now
unneeded ordinary kernel is released too. No native library or public host
interface is added; CGSE remains version 0.1.2.

Evidence and limits
-------------------
The original input changes from SEARCH_LIMIT at 41,016,800 observed work
(including compilation) to about 4.72M observed work at the same 40M search
cap. Original order and orders 17/1009, plus a four-worker fixed-budget run,
produce independently verified executable plans below 4.8M observed work.
The maintained guard is 8M; its constructive SAT expectation is unchanged.

The focused comparison covers 33 source/fuel inputs, 32 historical controls,
48 planted direct allocation models and 17 orders of the previous execution
regression. The source/fuel result improves from 20 to 21 verified plans, with
12 still UNKNOWN. The 20 previous successes, all 32 controls, all 48 direct
models and the 17 previous orders retain exactly the same results and observed
work. Build, packaged-engine tests, Forge/AE dump artifacts and all 1,478
baseline binary API entries pass. This is not the complete corpus or live Forge/AE
runtime test. The other source/fuel limits and larger allocations outside the
existing specialization's admission remain separate work.

CountLatticeEnumerationTest includes 48 small boxes checked by independent
original-coordinate enumeration, positive and empty scopes, side rows with
80-bit coefficients, 70-bit offsets, the stalled historical residual, one-unit
grants, cross-worker continuation, cancellation, memory refusal and numerical
refusal on a valid integer basis with out-of-range factors. Existing affine,
allocation, continuation, ownership and missing-material tests also apply.

Failed prototypes are retained in local case storage. Repeated projection
toward the box made no hard-case gain and substantially slowed one cheap
decomposition. Pure rational sphere search spent its opportunity on too few
nodes; numerical sphere search without quantity-box pruning also failed this
input. The retained implementation combines enumeration with box pruning.

First-bin follow-up (baseline 7df9bf67)
-------------------------------------
Allowing this search only after a completed first bin missed otherwise useful
reduced bases. With the bounded first-bin admission above, 48 planted direct
allocation models improve from 36 to 45 verified witnesses; the same three
models remain unresolved. Each newly solved original uses less than 1.32M
observed work instead of exhausting the specialist's 4M local allowance.
All previous direct successes retain or reduce their observed work.

The nine recovered models have constructive exact integer oracles. They also
pass signed 70-bit offsets and tiny-grant handoffs; eight of nine additional
shuffled models are solved. Seed 5's shuffled model remains unresolved on both
versions, despite its constructive SAT oracle. It must not be described as
infeasible or silently counted as a successful bounded search.

Embedding the same 27 variants in primitive recipes and running the main
integer-count portfolio improves 3 -> 26 independently checked executable
witnesses at the unchanged 40M request cap. All 26 are below 1.73M observed
work; the maintained test guards them below 3M, including original, shifted
and solved shuffled variants. The remaining permutation stays UNKNOWN.

The 33 source/fuel graph cases retain 21 verified executable plans and 12
limits. One existing fuel plan drops from 1,739,968 to 1,483,817 observed work;
the earlier 40-group recovery stays below 4.72M. Broad first-bin admission
without the size guard made that recovery about 6% more expensive, so the
larger first bins keep their old route. The separate remaining 40-group first
allocation and larger structures still need other improvements.

The joint box check has 96 additional original-coordinate exhaustive oracles
in three through six dimensions. These use a complete integer zero-sum
lattice with closed-form Gram factors, original side constraints and both
feasible and empty boxes. They do not assume that numerical pruning can
certify infeasibility of a caller's original model.

Reference
---------
FPLLL's public enumeration interface documents enumeration with Gram data,
target coordinates, a distance limit and pruning:
https://fplll.github.io/fplll/classEnumeration.html
https://fplll.github.io/fplll/classPruner.html

This implementation is independently written for CGSE's bounded candidate
search. It does not import FPLLL code, claim its pruning guarantees or use
numerical absence as a global proof. The original inputs, native comparisons,
failed trials, source snapshots and reproduction commands stay in the local
case archive; player evidence is not included in repository changes.
