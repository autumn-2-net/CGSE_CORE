Seed preservation and fragmented growth loops
============================================

PRESERVE also applies to a cycle input which the selected word consumes net.
Leaving only nonnegative-net prefix loans is insufficient: an order can deliver
its target while consuming every remaining representation of a productive loop.

SeedStartupGuard checks the candidate's funded final inventory, after delivery,
against the available producer catalog. Its optimistic closure treats terminal
inputs as replenishable and enabled recipes as unlimited, without spending their
inputs. A still-closed cyclic deficit therefore needs a retained startup form.
An open closure is only a necessary-condition check, not a recovery witness or
a proof that the retained seed is minimal. Finite startup loans are propagated
before requesting another representation of the same loop.

The available catalog is separate from the recipes bound into the execution
word. Stock pruning must not turn an unused growth recipe's output into a raw
material. Exclusions still apply; an unchanged returned input is not a net
producer. A bounded exact rational certificate exempts dissipative conversion
components and pure conservative conversions without net coproducts. Growing,
coproduct-producing and unsupported components keep the ordinary check. Explicit
final reserves remain mandatory in every mode.

The startup producer index is immutable and belongs to its compiler snapshot.
It contains no stock, request exclusions or prior acceptance decisions. Only a
completed index may be shared; cancelled construction stays private. Index
residency is bounded and included in compiler cache metrics. Closure explores
only backward dependencies of consumed inputs, propagates enabled producers
immediately and stops when all queried inputs have a supply. Net-zero return
recipes are indexed separately so a productive coproduct cannot disappear from
the conservative-conversion check.

A second exact certificate handles finite state transitions with several
inputs: total internal tokens may not increase, and any positive external
coproduct must strictly spend an internal token. This permits a finite state
machine to finish without preserving its control token. If neither this nor
the rational conversion certificate applies, the ordinary check remains.

Positive-only stock scouts may defer startup checking while they already have
other deficits. They still check the complete catalog before proposing a
feasible word. Refinement carries only newly discovered startup loans, not
temporary working catalysts from an abandoned word. Forked scouts own separate
reserve maps. Public missing previews retain their full publication checks.
The completed solve retains this loan provenance before closing its assembly,
so refinement receives the startup requirement even after workspace cleanup.

Physical restoration carries the full available catalog and the request's
excluded recipes separately from the bindings used by the word. An excluded
raw source cannot certify that a consumed startup material is replenishable.
For ordinary terminal consumption, the existing immutable producer index can
prove that no net-consumed produced input exists. That bounded check avoids
rebuilding the startup closure without weakening its acceptance conditions.

Candidate assembly, stock views, plan reuse, seed optimization and fallback
publication share this condition. Refill pruning and prefix supply also check
the resulting word. Temporary infinite cycle purchases used while searching
are removed before publication: the final check uses real external supply and
the stated finite bill. An empty prefix or terminal-only bill does not bypass
the PRESERVE contract. Delivery itself consumes inventory: a target can lose its
startup token even when the word has zero or positive net output, or is empty.

Restricted captured-domain proposals also need the original producer domain,
including unused recipes and input alternatives. Their publication check builds
a bounded ancestor domain and checks each returned alternative independently.
Concrete state transitions use the actual family predicates and entities;
matching family names alone never establishes equivalent physical states.
An unfinished domain check declines the proposal without claiming infeasibility.
Ordinary tool wear can be exempted only after closing a bounded exact return
domain and excluding feedback through manufacturing inputs and return products.
The declared capacity is a guarantee, not a proof that unseen states descend.

When count scheduling discovers a missing startup loan, CountDemandSearch can
complete that proposal's existing integer counts against locally tightened
material goals. Failure restores the original rows, goals, counts and allocation
continuation. This does not mutate a compiler cache or make a proposal-specific
loan mandatory for all recipe choices. Exact prefix, final stock and production
verification still decide whether the candidate can be published.

Ordinary fallback can repair a directly selected positive self-loop with a
bounded prefix. That child cannot recursively launch cyclic/source/boundary
search. The funded incumbent exists before the optional repair. Reusing an
earlier startup closure requires the same immutable suffix/domain and
componentwise nondecreasing produced-material final inventory and delta; other
changes run a new check. Candidate lifecycle cleanup releases the added loan
workspace on both acceptance and rejection.

Runtime suffix replacement cannot downgrade an existing PRESERVE order to
ordinary consumption. The mode check precedes inventory, obligation and program
mutation; explicit recovery ownership must also survive replacement. Ordinary
orders may opt into preservation, and same-mode replanning remains available.

Execution growth
----------------
SequenceLoopIndex recognizes repeated sequence prefixes incrementally, including
identity Repeat wrappers, without replacing saved PlanStep identities. The job
shares a 65,536-work/storage admission cap; each tick performs at most 1,024
indexing work. Restored cursors align with original period boundaries. This
recognition never adds recipe firings or changes the saved word.

PlanSourceCycles is an optional planning-side transformation for fragmented
source cycles with different batch sizes. It emits a repeat plus exact leftover
firings only when original counts and net deltas are unchanged, every prefix is
funded, and no larger initial inventory is required. It also checks long-valued
execution headroom, including target delivery and retained seeds. Components
with incoming selected producers or consumed configuration inputs decline the
transformation. Large/deep domains and local cutoffs retain the original word.
After extracting a source cycle, an acyclic suffix may combine its scattered
firings in producer order under the same bounded, exact inventory proof. This
avoids fixing source growth while leaving dozens of small downstream passes.

The two mechanisms address different representations. Recognizing equal
periods alone cannot combine varying completed passes. Neither mechanism
promises globally optimal throughput, and a newly planned safe word may require
more work than an earlier word that consumed its last seed. Per-dispatch
configuration consumption may differ when the number of pushes changes;
ordinary material conservation and declared seed refunds remain mandatory.

Structural refill proposals
---------------------------
CountDemandProjection reduces sparse single-output affine structure, solves a
bounded numerical proposal and repairs/checks every original integer row.
CountReverseSchedule provides another physical order for a verified count
vector. RefillTerminalSearch uses these as a separately bounded proposal with
only true terminal purchases, restores all original bindings and verifies the
complete word before offering it to the existing refill frontier. Incomparable
material bills remain alternatives. A cutoff, numerical failure or failed
schedule is not an infeasibility proof and does not discard the incumbent.
