Retained LP candidate search after packing repair (0.1.6)

Problem and service ownership
-----------------------------
The old warm-LP prototype could find local witnesses, but replacing the wide
exchange or consuming its unused grant immediately displaced successful PB
repairs. Merely keeping the same total cap did not preserve those outcomes.

CountPackingCore now remembers a completed, unsuccessful wide exchange's
projection and unused allowance. The PB repair receives its original service
first. Only when inconclusive PB hands back to the retained enumeration can
the saved projection receive one LP candidate attempt. The enumeration stack,
private conflict snapshot and original exterior remain available afterward.

The first attempt in this campaign still ran LP before PB completion: it
recovered mknapcb3_01_bks but lost mknapcb2_03_bks. That scheduling variant
remains private evidence and is not the shipped behavior.

No request, core or repair cap increases. Only a completed wide exchange with
at least 262144 unused work can enqueue the extra method. Its remaining
allowance is captured once and deferred; repeated slices, observations and
worker changes cannot refill it. Expiry or optional memory refusal returns to
the original enumeration. No native solver is a runtime dependency.

Candidate algorithm and exact boundary
--------------------------------------
CountPackingLpSearch admits at most 64 Boolean columns and 35 input rows.
It builds a bounded primal relaxation from positive capacity rows and one
negative profit demand. Fractional branching then retains a parent basis and
uses dual reoptimization after fixing a coordinate. A depth's tableau,
primal point, reduced costs and basis buffers are reused for later siblings.
The bounded worst-case depth workspace is reserved before allocation.

LP prices propose an upper bound only. Nonnegative integer price weights
reconstruct it with BigInteger over the current fixed Boolean box. Arbitrary
finite prices are permitted; LP optimality is not a premise of that bound.
Floating point statuses, iteration limits and unsuccessful branches are
never exposed as face infeasibility or a global proof.

Every proposed bit mask is checked against every original projected row,
including mixed-sign rows omitted from the numerical relaxation. Admission
bounds absolute activity before exact long summation. The parent then
restores fixed exterior columns and checks its original BigInteger rows;
the normal planner still verifies primitive execution, reservation and target.

This retains bases within one proposed face. Its root LP is rebuilt after
exterior substitution: it does not yet project the full-model basis, retain
bases across orders, or exhaust the full external Boolean space. The current
fractional branching rule also differs from the archived sensitivity variant.
Unsuccessful local proposals keep the original whole-model outcome unknown.

Validation scope
----------------
The maintained CountPackingLpSearchTest independently enumerates 192 small
original domains: all 128 positive controls retain exact witnesses. Arbitrary
price vectors across 1536 fixed boxes yield 306 exact prunes, each checked
against every original Boolean assignment. It also covers row scaling,
values beyond double's exact-integer range, signed bit 63, additional mixed
rows, worker handoffs, optional refusal, interrupted allocation, reusable
sibling buffers and the owning core's deferred service contract.

The actual 0.1.6 library, compared with eb5589ea on all 296 canonical packing
inputs at 40M work, 256MiB and no wall timeout, improves 146 -> 147 positives
and 150 -> 149 unresolved, without losing an old positive. The newly recovered
mknapcb3_01_bks returns a fully checked plan at 38,862,924 reported work.
It has 500 choices and five capacities. Its reserved peak is 95,601,171 bytes;
the previous unresolved request peaked at 39,930,404 bytes. Solving and restoring
the whole plan is not a claim of lower memory than the former budget exit.

Of the 146 old positives, 145 retain identical work. mknapcb6_09_bks increases
from 36,124,022 to 37,459,325 work and still satisfies its existing 38M guard.
Old-positive aggregate work rises 0.124%, and every old-positive reserved peak
is unchanged. This is additional solving capacity, not a universal speedup.

Three cases, eight recipe orders, serial/fixed-four-worker scheduling and both
libraries give 96 comparative requests. All 32 old positive requests remain
positive. The new case improves in seven of eight orders in each mode; order97
remains unresolved in both. The 14 verified new requests are mandatory in
execution-boundaries.json at 40M with a 39M observed-work guard; this raises its
required request inventory from 733 to 747 without duplicating source data.

Twenty-five related maintained programs, eight deliberate fault injections,
the actual-library checks, offline assemble, 1478 ABI entries, source-JAR
correspondence and Forge/AE-dump packaging pass. All process trees use the
same four logical CPUs. Some validation jobs overlap, so their wall times
are not used as isolated performance measurements.

These results and all unsuccessful prototypes are retained in the local case
registry. This increment is not a fresh execution of all 20927 historical
records, all 747 execution boundaries or a new native-solver run. Historical
native witnesses and unrelated equality/allocation statuses retain their scopes.
