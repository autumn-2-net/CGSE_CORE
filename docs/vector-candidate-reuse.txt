Reusing integer allocation candidates (2026-10-18)
================================================

A valid first allocation need not have a feasible residual allocation. The
vector partition strategy previously closed the first bin's exact affine basis
as soon as it found one witness. An exhausted residual matcher then selected
another decomposition order and repeated elimination. The six fixed order
attempts could miss a feasible nearby first allocation. Expanding that order
list alone did not improve the remaining 33 source/fuel cases.

CountAffineLattice now supports a bounded candidate stream after a caller
rejects a partial allocation. It keeps the exact affine point, reduced basis
and Gram coefficients, and tries at most 32 deterministic interior/corner
targets with nearest-plane reconstruction. Every returned vector still passes
the original integer rows and domains. This is a candidate heuristic: target
points, rejected candidates and exhausted trials never become cuts or an
infeasibility certificate. Other callers keep their existing first-witness path.

CountVectorPartition retains one completed first-bin basis while checking the
residual. After that decomposition fails, it may ask the same basis for another
candidate before the ordinary allocation-order retry. This uses the same rows,
coordinate order and original multiplicities. No state is shared between
requests or different domains. The retained child is closed on success, refusal,
quota exhaustion, cancellation or request closure.

The extra branch receives at most 32,768 local work units and at most half the
work already spent when the opportunity is first priced. Candidate generation,
new residual solving and original-row verification all spend this one allowance.
It is checked at cooperative step boundaries, so a final atomic step may cross
the local threshold. Handoffs and further candidates never renew it. The parent
request's search and memory caps remain authoritative. A failed extra branch
continues the pending ordinary order retry; it does not replace a viable
ordinary frontier or export the residual failure as a global proof.

Comparison against d15bd4a9
---------------------------
At the unchanged 40M search cap, the 33 historical source/fuel inputs improve
from 18 verified plans / 15 UNKNOWN to 20 verified plans / 13 UNKNOWN. The two
recovered inputs are alias/shuffle forms of one recipe family:

  manual_fuel_n24_c3_d2_a2_s107_aliases  41,607,354 -> 9,377,075 work
  manual_fuel_n24_c3_d2_a2_s107_shuffle  41,133,189 -> 8,977,017 work

Reported totals include compilation; the old search itself exhausted 40M.
Their six original/order variants and two four-worker fixed-budget runs all
produce independently verified primitive-prefix/target witnesses below 10M
observed work. The maintained boundary guard is 12M observed work at the
original 40M request cap. UNKNOWN cases keep their constructive SAT labels.

All 18 earlier successes remain solved. Their summed work is 143,048,074 ->
141,959,816, with a worst individual increase of 1.96%. This is not a claim of
monotonic speedup. An additional 48 planted direct allocation models retain
all 35 old witnesses and recover seed 29 (4M local limit -> 61,337 work).
Some cheap direct decompositions still pay bounded extra work; the worst
observed increase is 31,405 work (27.38%) on the 114,717-work seed 25.
The additional 32 historical controls all remain solved (413,616,246 ->
410,169,708 summed work). All 17 orders of the earlier execution-regression
case retain exactly the same result and observed work as d15bd4a9.

An earlier unpriced 131,072-work prototype solved another synthetic seed but
more than doubled one cheap case. Its failed cost behavior is kept as a
negative control; it is not the default admission rule. Two variants that
deferred reuse until after the fixed order list did not improve the 33 inputs.
Their source snapshots and original logs remain in local case storage.

The focused checks cover candidate streams, exact large/signed arithmetic,
budget/worker continuation, memory refusal, cancellation, existing affine and
portfolio paths, missing-material expansion, prior execution regressions and
packaged engine/ABI checks. These are focused comparisons, not a complete
historical corpus or live Forge/AE execution run. Private incident evidence
and failed experimental runs remain outside the repository.
