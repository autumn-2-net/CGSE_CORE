Missing material expansion from verified execution requirements
==============================================================

A valid refill witness proves that its listed supplies fund an execution. It
does not prove that every listed supply is a terminal material. The former
forward preview could stop at a depth/alternative limit, or borrow an earlier
input after a later sibling consumed it. Verification accepted those loans
because they funded the word, leaving craftable intermediates in the UI.

MissingMaterialExpansion refines the selected, verified witness in both main
planning and ordinary fallback. Its prefix phase preserves the execution suffix,
recipe bindings and final seed obligations. For a prepended batch of n firings,
each resource's new requirement is exactly

  max(input + max(0, input - output) * (n - 1), old_need - net_output * n).

This sparse update retains coproduct credits, returned tools and ordering. It
does not recursively copy the whole suffix. Counts remain BigInteger values;
large batches use the existing compact PlanStep representation. A forced-order
prefix must also replace any finished-target units it consumes, so existing
target stock cannot replace the suffix's verified production credit.

RefillProducerOrder indexes only the deficit's reachable producer support.
Finite derivations from terminal supplies receive increasing ranks. Iterative
SCC analysis ranks a remaining component above its outside feedstocks, with
the same rank for all members of a cycle. An ordinary prefix must reduce the
selected deficit and can increase only strictly lower-ranked deficits. This
prevents alternating loans around a cycle. A real startup seed can remain;
an ordinary item downstream of that cycle is expanded to the actual seed.
An available raw escape route is expanded even if another producer cycles.
Productive cycles may reduce seed loans while expanding their feedstocks.

The rank index factors each reached recipe's input support once. Material
nodes keep their original producer lists, while a shared recipe AND node
owns readiness and reached positive-net outputs. A p-output/q-input recipe
therefore retains O(p+q) incidence instead of copying q inputs p times. It
does not discover unrelated coproducts or erase a returned tool/self-input
enablement requirement. External supplies and excluded sources keep their
existing boundaries.

Completed sources merge later coproduct outputs by original incidence order.
This preserves material ranks even when first-ready finite derivations depend
on queue order. Single-output sources emit directly without a merge queue.
An unshared sole producer lends its input list directly to material SCC
traversal, avoiding an extra vertex on ordinary chains and simple cycles.
SCC analysis traverses the factored graph: recipe-only components add zero
material depth, and a component containing materials adds one. Cyclic members
share a rank above outside feedstocks, as in the projected material graph.

Discovery, readiness propagation, DFS, component extraction, outside-depth
inspection and rank publication use saved cursors. A rank step visits at most
64 operations before returning to its caller. This is a cooperative work
bound, not a wall-time guarantee against GC, host key operations or scheduling.
The extra recipe-vertex state has a cost for narrow single-output catalogs;
wide joint producers are the intended memory/work improvement. A completed
rank index keeps no inventory-dependent or cross-request mutable state.

For a growth producer with input a, available stock s, current requirement R
and positive net output d, the old ceil(deficit/d) count can run past the
startup requirement plateau. Expansion also evaluates the exact breakpoint

  max(1, ceil(max(0, R - max(a, s)) / d)).

The old count stays available: its extra coproducts or forced-target credit
can matter. Candidates are compared by their entire before-requirement
vectors, including unchanged resources, and by forced-target production
credit. A strictly componentwise better candidate replaces the selected one;
incomparable candidates keep catalog priority. The existing preference for
a fully stock-funded producer remains. No item/fluid exchange rate or sum
of unlike missing quantities is used. Candidate scratch memory is charged
and released even when comparison or evaluation exhausts a budget.

After the established quality stages have completed, RefillSourceSearch can
compare a few alternate producers after full expansion to terminal supply.
It uses the verified incumbent's delivery, seeds, bindings and production
credit, shares a completed rank index between bounded source trials, and
publishes only a verified componentwise improvement in missing supply.
First-ready ranks can be too shallow for an alternate path: a private trial
may cross that order once per preferred material, consuming the exception
when it applies the prefix. Ordinary expansion keeps the original guard.
Cycles cannot renew a consumed exception or turn a failed trial into a
negative proof. Nested seed subrequests and existing cyclic archives decline
this extra pass. After unused-source trials, a separate bounded continuation
can redistribute counts between physically compatible bound producers.
It evaluates a small Cartesian product of preferences, so changing one route
need not reset other incumbent mixtures to worse defaults. At most eight
retained hints form at most eighteen complete candidate words. Fully completed
support may be reused; interrupted discovery cannot rule out a bound mixture.
Complete discovery with no old bound alternatives skips this continuation.
Both arms retain complete verified publications across limits and accept only
strict componentwise improvements in missing supply. See
review-0.1.5-followup.txt for quotas, costs, production and per-push restrictions,
validation, and remaining joint-count limitations.

RefillWordPruning optionally reduces excessive counts already in the completed
word. It works backward from delivery and final seeds, tracking inventory at
each boundary of the ORIGINAL prefix. Each positive net output d requires at
least ceil(max(0, shortened_suffix_need - prefix_inventory) / d) executions.
The maximum of these bounds, followed by exact backward prefix requirements,
preserves a legal word within the original initial-supply vector. Shrinking a
nonpositive-net resource's count cannot worsen its startup/total requirement.
This goal-aware rewrite may discard unrequested final surplus; the generic
PlanFlowPruning substitution still preserves every original net output because
it can operate inside an unknown surrounding word.

The original bound recipes and order stay fixed. Useful coproducts and final
seeds enter the backward need, including seeds not touched by any recipe. Forced
orders retain their previous net target credit; orders needing a separate
indivisible-count production proof are left unchanged. Consumed configuration
inputs also decline rewriting, preserving their per-push contract. Sequences
use explicit cursors; composite repeat bodies stay opaque and their internal
order is never flattened into independent batches. Nested exact counts larger
than long remain compact. Self-returning recipes trigger this pass directly.
Otherwise an optional structural scan looks for an output of a previously
consumed material in the actual execution word. This also selects multi-recipe
feedback such as A -> B -> more A; catalog order is not an execution order.
Positive repeats visit their body once, regardless of count, and zero-count
branches are skipped. Saved word/input/output cursors use bounded slices and
charged scratch memory. The scan stops after at most min(1024, max(128,
allowance/4)) work units plus its exit check, retaining the old bill. It is an
applicability hint, not a cycle proof or a claim that an unselected bill is best.

Both initial withdrawal and missing supply must be componentwise no larger.
A separate complete prefix/delivery/seed verification precedes publication.
When the prefix pass just verified this exact immutable word and binding tuple,
its completed summary is borrowed within the same request. The owning verifier
keeps its memory charge until the child closes; unrelated words and terminal-only
bills use their own fold. This avoids repeating the original-word verification.
The old verified bill remains available during refinement and on work/time/
memory exits; cancellation propagates. Scratch memory is charged and released.
The word-reduction and cyclic-supply verifiers use PlanVerification.refill: the
same complete exact fold and prefix/delivery/final-seed checks, without building
recipe multiplicities and gross physical-output totals that these callers never
read. Their existing net target-credit guards remain in place. This internal
entry is not a forced-crafting proof; count/output accessors reject unavailable
metadata. Public verification and its low-net-gain ForceCraftProof path retain
the full production bookkeeping. RefillVerificationTest compares both modes on
literal funded words and deficient-prefix/goal/seed counterexamples, shared and
huge words, returned tokens, limits and scheduler handoff. Prefix expansion also
omits unused metadata after its mandatory expansion/fold/key checks have spent
2048 work units: the later work-scaled quality allowance is then already at its
8192 ceiling. Below that threshold it retains the original count pass and hence
the small-preview scheduling allowance. The remaining-work cap still applies;
saved work can make more of that existing allowance available. No prefix, target
or final-seed validation is skipped. Threshold equality, prior request work and
deficient witnesses are covered by RefillVerificationTest.
The work cap is at most 8192 units and one quarter of remaining work (with a
single bounded continuation of overshoot), scaled down for small expansions.
Plain cursor slices visit at most 64 entries; child folds/verifiers own their
slices. Terminal-only missing bills can enter this optional pass too, without
rebuilding the producer graph or losing the bill at an exhausted entry boundary.
Words with no observed feedback decline before folding the whole word.

Single-recipe units read their immutable input/output ports directly during the
backward support and requirement passes. They no longer build required/delta/peak
maps for every occurrence. Input ports are visited first, then outputs not already
visited as inputs; duplicate returned outputs still consume a bounded cursor
visit. Zero-net returned tools therefore keep their startup requirement. Port
differences fit in long, while repetition counts and requirements remain exact
BigIntegers. Composite units still use their complete ordered summary. No cache,
cache-preparation work or additional refinement allowance is introduced. The
final independent prefix/delivery/seed verification is unchanged.

After this pass itself has consumed 2048 work units, ordinary cursor handoffs
combine empty input/output boundaries, flat-batch unwrapping and emission with
their adjacent charged visits. Each atom/hierarchy descent and each material
port is still visited within the 64-unit cooperative slice; there is no unbounded
port loop. Earlier scheduling is retained so small work-scaled preview allowances
do not shrink. The saved visits stay inside the original request/refinement quota.
Zero target deltas also avoid unused exact multiplication/addition.

RefillUnitTraversalTest replays repeated words with positive/negative/zero net
ports, catalog/input order reversal, final seeds, mixed direct/composite units,
long-limit quantities and cutoffs. It also requires wide repeated words to reach
their one-firing conservation bound within the existing 8192-work allowance when
the enclosing verifier already owns the original fold, including constrained
memory and release of all private workspace. This improves completed witnesses
within the quota; it does not guarantee that every bounded pass will finish.

After the original per-batch pass completes, RefillWordBlocks can recognize
exact adjacent repetitions of 2..16 atoms in its retained execution word. It
keeps every batch's recipe, count and order; it neither rearranges interleavings
nor enumerates opaque repeat bodies. Batch equality compares only recipe/count;
composite equality uses identity without recursive equals/hashCode. Separate
occurrences of a shared node remain separate executions. Grouping creates an
ordered composite Repeat, then invokes the same verified backward reduction
with grouping disabled in that child. This lets an entire A -> B -> more A
round shrink even when a constituent alone would lack its returned startup/tool.

The completed per-batch result is published before grouping starts. Both initial
and missing vectors of the child are bounded by that incumbent, not merely by
the original larger bill. Cutoffs retain it, and cancellation propagates. The
scan starts only with at least four retained flat batches and 512 units left in
the SAME pruning allowance, uses at most min(2048, remaining/2) charged visits,
and leaves the rest for the child. No extra request allowance or recursive
fixed-point loop is introduced. Cursor turns visit at most 64 entries and child
folds/verifiers run in separate turns. A just-completed incumbent verification
can lend its exact fold because regrouping preserves the execution trace; the
final reduced candidate is always verified afresh. Scratch and retained word
memory stay charged until child close. RefillWordBlocksTest checks literal
batch traces, conservation, tool returns, seeds/coproducts, forced credit,
configuration exclusions, memory/time/work/cancel exits and scheduler handoff.

After the parent's 2048-work scheduling floor, the short scan can maintain an
optional 15-atom lookahead index. Only periods whose first atoms are exactly equal
need a full block comparison. Masks retain every occurrence, including duplicates
across the ring boundary; they do not select a different period or authorize a
match. Exact grouping order is identical to the unindexed scan when both finish.
Each insertion/eviction is a charged continuation visit. A completed block can
jump past the window without traversing that gap. Batch keys use recipe/count;
opaque composites use identity, never recursive equality or a hash-only match.

The index reserves 4096 bytes only when that is at most one sixteenth of available
workspace. Refusal keeps the original scan. Allocation pressure drops the index
before group storage, and scan completion/limits release it before child work.
This accelerates failed first-atom comparisons within the existing scan allowance;
it does not increase any request, pruning or cache-preparation budget.

An optional 16-atom sliding index can prove the short scan has no match. Every
exact period2..16 tandem necessarily has equal atoms within that distance. The
index observes all retained atoms during their already charged emission; only
the completed stream can certify absence. Any nearby equality, including distance
one, conservatively keeps the short scan. Batch equality uses recipe/count and
opaque composites use identity, matching the exact recognizer. Hash collisions
are not equality. No second word traversal or cross-request cache is introduced.
Admission requires at least 4096 pruning allowance and more than 16 bound recipes;
these are cost guards, not correctness assumptions. The fixed index reserves
2304 bytes only when that fits within one sixteenth of available workspace.
Reservation refusal keeps the original scan. A possible match releases the index
early; primary allocation pressure or a composite child fold discards it. A
completed negative certificate releases it before candidate construction and
verification. Optional information cannot displace those primary allocations.

If that short-period scan produces no complete grouping, or the completed index
proves no short match exists, a second bounded scan
can use repeated atoms as anchors for longer tandem blocks. It runs only with
at least 34 retained atoms and 1024 units left in the SAME pruning allowance.
The original per-batch result keeps priority, and any possible short match still
gets its original scan first. Anchor scanning receives at most min(2048, remaining/2)
visits; reduction and verification use the rest. It never extends the request
or pruning quota and never recursively invokes another grouping pass.

Recognizing a short block is not itself a supply reduction: it can be a necessary
inner loop within a reducible outer round. If the short-group child returns its
input plan unchanged, the parent closes that failed attempt and may use the same
remaining allowance for one larger-period sibling. This also applies when the
nearest-anchor scan only grouped short loops. The sibling requires period >=17,
so it cannot re-enter this continuation through another short block. An improving
child always keeps priority. A cutoff still returns the completed incumbent.
This is a bounded choice of passes, not repeated retries until the budget grows.

Each atom's nearest equal predecessor proposes a period. The entire ordered
block and every extension are then compared exactly, including recipe IDs and
batch counts. Hash collisions cannot authorize grouping. Opaque composite nodes
use identity and are not unrolled or compared recursively. Unmatched prefixes,
tails, zero batches and separate occurrences keep their exact execution order.
For the larger-period sibling, a too-near duplicate retains its previous anchor
until the minimum distance is reached. Otherwise an inner repetition could keep
overwriting the start of an outer block, even when two full copies exist. The
original nearest-predecessor policy remains unchanged for the first anchor scan.
This remains an incomplete hint: it does not retain all eligible predecessors,
and an interrupted scan publishes no partial regrouping.

The resulting compact word uses the existing exact backward reduction and final
verification. If no preceding reduction changed the original plan, its externally
owned verified summary may also serve the long-block child; the owner remains
alive and charged throughout. After the 2048-work scheduling floor, an unchanged
original word can lend the fold to a short-group child too. That exact grouping
can also inherit the parent's completed recipe/feedback admission and cursor
scheduling floor. It need not rescan unchanged recipe bindings or re-establish
the same structural hint. Small refinements keep their prior scheduling, and
every changed word still receives complete final verification. These are local
immutable facts, with no cross-request stock or recipe cache.
Both initial and missing supply still must be
componentwise bounded by the completed incumbent. Time/work/memory limits retain
that incumbent, while cancellation propagates. Anchors and candidate word storage
are reserved and released; each cursor turn visits at most 64 entries. The extra
optional search can consume more of the existing allowance and workspace.

Segmented words and exact local structural identity
--------------------------------------------------

An outer loop can consist of several Repeat(Sequence(...), n) segments instead
of flat batches. Two previous admission/recognition rules missed this case: the
grouping gate counted only flat batches, and opaque nodes compared by object
identity. Independently compiled equal segments could therefore keep unnecessary
rounds and their feed bill, even though the supplied execution word was valid.

After existing identity-based grouping fails to improve the bill, a word with
at least two opaque atoms and four total atoms may spend remaining quality work
on exact local hash-consing. Opaque-only words can enter this continuation too.
The pass requires at least 1024 work left and receives at most min(2048, left/2).
It uses the original pruning allowance, runs once, and cannot recursively start
another grouping search. Previously successful grouping retains its priority.

RefillWordShapes interns exact batch recipe/count, repeat body/count, and ordered
sequence-child identities. Child lists use constant-sized cons keys. Traversal
is iterative and charged in slices of at most 64 visits; it does not recursively
hash a composite or enumerate repeat occurrences. Hash collisions require exact
key equality. Distinct recipe IDs, order, zero operations, sequence nesting and
physical batch boundaries remain distinct. This is structural identity within
one bound recipe catalog, not an algebraic rewrite or cross-request cache.

The optional workspace is capped at min(256 KiB, available workspace/16) on entry.
Allocation refusal or a work/time limit retains the already verified incumbent;
cancellation still propagates. No partial identity mapping is published. The
private traversal/intern tables are released before regrouping and verification;
only the charged result references live until the owning continuation closes.
All candidates still pass the existing exact supply, prefix, target-credit and
final-seed checks. An identity-only negative short-block hint is not reused after
canonicalization changes the atoms.

Mixed-source stock allocation is an optional improvement of the completed bill.
While inspecting ordinary candidates, expansion detects positive partial batches
that introduce no additional deficits. Only if such a hint remains useful after
whole-source selection does a second bounded pass try these batch counts. Every
whole-source candidate is still considered before taking a partial source, so a
later wholly funded producer keeps its priority.

For an input with amount a, net loss l > 0, suffix requirement R and allowable
initial ceiling C = stock + existing deficit, a no-added-deficit batch must obey

  n <= 1 + floor((C - a) / l), and n <= floor((C - R) / l).

If a > C there is no positive funded batch. Returned inputs with zero net loss
still require their startup amount. External inputs omit the inventory bound;
forced-target consumption additionally respects the existing production credit.
The minimum of these exact bounds is evaluated again through the normal complete
support/rank checks. All counts remain BigInteger, including amounts beyond long.
Input scanning is resumable and does not enumerate firings or durability states.
The alternative borrows the completed producer ranking and source lists from its
parent: compiler, missing roots, external supplies and exclusions are identical,
and no ranking data is mutated. The parent alone owns and releases that workspace.
This avoids repeating graph discovery/SCC work for the optional search.

Partial funding alone is not a quality guarantee: a greedy choice can consume
stock needed by another branch, or lose a useful coproduct. Therefore the ordinary
expanded, verified plan is retained before attempting any mixture. The alternative
starts from the same original suffix, stock, exclusions and seed obligations; its
complete word is verified before comparing the final missing-material vectors.
It is accepted only when every missing quantity is no larger and at least one is
smaller. Equal or incomparable bills retain the original exact plan. This compares
new supplies, not an invented material price or equal initial-stock consumption.

The optional pass uses the remaining request budget after ordinary expansion. Its
local work allowance is the minimum of one quarter of remaining work, 8192 units,
and max(512, four times ordinary expansion work); a continuation can overshoot that
soft allowance by at most 64 operations. Fewer than 512 remaining units skip it.
It shares request time/cancellation/memory limits and never changes configured
main/fallback/cache allowances. The verified ordinary plan is available to an
owner interrupted during improvement. Work/time/memory exits retain that plan;
cancellation still propagates. The opportunity gate and cap limit extra work but
do not make this quality improvement free, or guarantee all possible mixtures.

After ordinary expansion, mixtures and word reduction, the root main planner may
use RefillSupplySearch to prepare a small cyclic input more cheaply. It borrows
the completed producer ranking, following cyclic inputs of the same rank. Each
neighborhood is limited to 12 recipes, 16 material keys and 128 original ports;
ordinary acyclic shortages never enter a coverability search. Discovery uses
saved cursors and respects excluded sources and existing physical bindings.
Consumed configuration recipes decline this optional search.

For each cyclic shortage m, a few strictly lower supplies (0, 1, floor(m/2), m-1)
are tried. Other keys retain the ceiling of current stock plus the completed
missing bill. The goals are the ORIGINAL suffix's initial requirements on every
touched key. Thus a searched prefix must restore what the existing suffix needs,
including resources shared with other branches and final seed reservations. A
forced target additionally has to retain its supplied quantity: prefix net target
consumption is rejected, even when the target is an external supply.

This reuses BackwardCoverability's exact antichain and original recipe actions in
a witness-only mode. A local cutoff or closed restricted search reports UNKNOWN,
does not enter the forward/PDR/induction portfolio, and exports no absence proof.
The original BackwardCoverability constructor retains its existing portfolio and
proof behavior. A constructor interrupted after reserving its first node now
releases that workspace before propagating the exception.

A discovered prefix is summarized, its exact requirements are composed with the
unchanged suffix, and the complete funded plan is verified. Only a strictly
componentwise smaller missing bill is accepted; available stock withdrawals may
increase. There is no exchange rate between materials and no artificial supply
of a new feedstock. The retained plan stays available during the search and at
parent publication limits. No partially checked prefix or restricted absence
result is published. Ordinary fallback does not enable this cyclic search.

After the primitive search retains its best completed bill, an optional second
pass supplies exact shortcut actions to the same antichain. LinearMacroCompilation
contracts single-input/single-output chains using integer throughput ratios.
Its words are expanded and summarized against original physical recipes, retaining
intermediate requirements and peaks. RefillCycleActions also composes a root-
consuming action with a root-producing action through a shared transfer key. If
the first produces a and the second consumes b, their multiplicities are b/gcd(a,b)
and a/gcd(a,b). Only positive-net-root round trips are proposed. Side inputs,
returned tools, coproducts and all intermediate keys stay in the exact summary.
Original primitive actions remain available for partial chains and interleavings.

The shortcut pass starts from the completed primitive-search bill, not the older
unrefined bill. Taking a different early witness can otherwise trade an already
eliminated deficit for less of another material. Both passes enforce componentwise
dominance and verify the complete expanded word before retaining it. Shortcut
construction visits at most512 pairing/key entries and adds at most8 round trips
per neighborhood. It is not a general cycle enumerator; compilation and summaries
are bounded by the same small neighborhood and remaining optional work.

Entry requires at least4096 remaining work units. Each of the two optional passes
uses at most min(8192, remaining/4) plus one continuation; the second starts only
with at least4096 remaining units and skips roots without shortcuts. This adds a
bounded improvement cost without reducing the original search's quota, while the
request's total work/time/memory caps stay unchanged. Each witness trial is capped
at2048 units. Glue traversals use64-operation slices; the reused antichain has its
own coarser slice, bounded by that trial cap, not a64-operation latency guarantee.
Neighborhood ports bound its synchronous setup; scratch and child workspaces are
charged. Work/time/memory limits keep the best verified bill; cancellation remains
exceptional. These are small candidate searches, not binary search proofs of
minimum supply or a complete multi-objective frontier.

After those searches retain their completed bill, RefillRepeatSizing may improve
the repetition count of an already witnessed prefix fragment. For its exact need r,
net change d and the retained suffix's need R, n positive copies require

  max(r + max(0, -d) * (n - 1), R - d * n).

With supply ceiling C = stock + retained shortage, each negative-delta coordinate
therefore caps n by both 1 + floor((C-r)/(-d)) and floor((C-R)/(-d)). A first-copy
requirement above C rejects the word. Positive-delta shortages plateau at
ceil(max(0, R-max(r,stock))/d); the largest such breakpoint, capped by every loss,
reduces the fixed word's shortages without adding another material. Zero-net tools,
external supplies, ignored target stock and forced production credit retain their
existing meanings. Counts and bounds stay exact beyond long.

This uses only prefix words accepted by this RefillSupplySearch instance. Saved
postorder cursors expose small sequence bodies before their repeated enclosing
words; repeats are never unrolled, shared words are visited once, and zero repeats
are skipped. The original suffix is not mined for hints or rewritten. The scan
freezes the earlier prefix chain before accepting further improvements, preventing
the newly synthesized prefixes from recursively becoming new hints. Each candidate
is composed with the latest retained bill and fully verified through the same
publication path. It cannot replace a shortage that an earlier pass eliminated.

Sizing starts only with a changed, still-missing bill and at least1024 remaining
work units. It uses at most min(4096, remaining/4) plus one continuation, scans at
most256 word entries and summarizes at most16 sequence bodies. Each sizing slice
visits at most32 material coordinates. It preserves the preceding searches' quotas
and results on work/time/memory exits; cancellation propagates. This is a bounded
fixed-word quantity refinement, not a new route search or global minimum proof.

Refill preparation, source selection, input/output support evaluation, full-vector
comparison, applying a selected prefix, reversing prefixes and building deficits
also retain cursors. Each expansion step performs at most 64 operations, or calls
one child rank/verification continuation on its own. A proposal remains private
until its support and comparison are complete; no next source sees a partially
applied requirement vector. Both retained proposals are released on interruption.
Iterator handoffs share their last port's work unit rather than charging another
arithmetic operation. Duplicate input/output support needs no temporary union set.

Publication transfers the completed private maps and flat step list into immutable
views, without copying the full vectors again for the funded verification plan.
These builders must never be mutated after transfer, including on close or another
step call. Public GraphPlan and PlanStep.Sequence constructors still defensively
copy and validate caller-owned collections. Public signatures are unchanged.

After expansion, PlanVerification checks the funded complete word again. A
work/time/memory exit does not publish a partially expanded bill; an already
completed ordinary bill remains available during optional improvement. Existing
main and fallback budgets remain independent and unchanged. Feasible plans
skip this pass. Incomplete capture stays conditional: expansion does not prove
missing recipe/state domains infeasible or claim globally minimal supplies.
Changed plans drop stale seed-optimality and alternative-plan metadata.

Final verification streams material keys from the completed exact summary, then
the target and independent seeds, in the same order as before. It no longer copies
a large union or rescans irrelevant initial inventory. The exact fold retains a
delta entry for every prefix requirement, including zero-net returned tools;
initial keys outside that set and outside the goals have zero need/change/goal
and their quantities were already validated as nonnegative. Up to 32 key visits
share one continuation, without an extra arithmetic charge for a yield boundary.

GraphRecipe records input/output overlap once at construction on the smaller port
side, combining the old input/configuration/reusable aggregation loops. Ordinary
batch folding reads this immutable fact instead of scanning recipe ports at every
batch boundary. Logical reusable returns still take the general fold, even when
they do not represent physical outputs. Construction remains linear in recipe
size; this does not introduce an inventory-dependent or cross-request cache.

Completed summary/count traversals relinquish their temporary indices without a
bulk clear loop. Only positive batches under positive repeat edges enter counting,
so every indexed recipe gets a positive propagated count and needs no final zero
filter. Zero batches/repeats, shared words and exact counts beyond long remain
covered by independent literal-count tests.

An interrupted preview must not erase an already completed original-domain
infeasibility proof. GraphPlanningWork retains that proof with an unavailable
preview diagnostic; otherwise the actual work/time/memory limit remains the
result. A fully verified expansion completed before its parent's publication
boundary is also retained. Capture and request owners still prevent a local
proof from escaping an incomplete or restricted recipe domain, including in
diagnostic text. Cancellation remains exceptional, not an infeasibility result.

Bounded supply alternatives
---------------------------

After the existing main-planner prefix and quantity passes, a fourth optional
pass can retain seed/feed tradeoffs in GraphPlan.alternatives(). For example,
with A + feed -> B, B -> 2 A and 10 A -> goal, the primary bill B=5 can coexist
with verified bills B=4, feed=2 and B=1, feed=8. No resource price chooses between
these incomparable bills. Ordinary fallback keeps its existing cheaper path.

The pass reuses completed support ranks, exact linear-chain/round-trip actions,
backward material expansion and full funded plan verification. It does not build
another antichain solver. For each selected growth word it tries one repetition
and the exact quantity reaching that root's startup plateau. New feed deficits
use the captured catalog and exclusions to expand toward terminal materials or
cycle boundaries; that child cannot recursively search more tradeoffs. Physical
bindings, returned tools, target production credit and final seeds are preserved.

Only eight proposed words and four alternatives are retained. This pass starts
after the older passes have used their own allowances, requires at least 4096
remaining work units, and receives min(8192, remaining/8) plus at most one bounded
continuation. Existing seed alternatives are preserved by declining this pass.
The per-key missing/initial/seed order matches SeedPortfolio's dominance rule;
comparison scans yield after at most 64 charged visits. Publication shares
immutable maps and execution words, and all new builder memory stays charged
while candidates are retained. Work/time/memory cutoffs keep the last completed
primary and archive; cancellation propagates.

A strictly smaller componentwise missing bill, a newly feasible plan, or a full
dominance improvement can become primary. Incomparable candidates only become
alternatives. Duplicate or dominated bills are discarded; a full archive retains
earlier incomparable entries. This is neither a complete Pareto front nor a
minimum-supply certificate. If the pairwise shortcuts have no growth word for an
entry, a bounded completion follows the old candidates. It balances one cyclic
transfer at a time using exact integer throughput ratios and composes the actual
execution words. Existing immutable component summaries are reused. Thus an
entry hidden inside a linear contraction can gain a three-or-more-component
route, such as return -> make -> bridge, without a new antichain search.

Completion shares the same remaining pass allowance and offer/archive limits.
Its new words also reuse the exact stock-funded repeat breakpoint after their
one-run and startup-plateau candidates. The earlier sizing pass could not see
these words; this interior candidate avoids leaving usable feed stock idle.
It visits at most 1024 cursor positions, forms at most 32 compositions, retains
at most 32 partial paths of depth below 12, and returns at most four growth words.
A path uses each component at most once; integer multiplicities stay in its word.
Existing round trips and startup requirements already at one skip completion.
Only the completed cyclic support supplies transfer keys. This covers selected
rotations, not all interleavings or arbitrary integer circulations. No missing-path
or infeasibility proof follows from exhausting this restricted search.

RefillTradeoffTest independently replays primitive firings for mixed supplies,
craftable feeds, seeds, returned tools and coproducts, including catalog reversal.
It checks exact totals beyond long, exclusions and binding/configuration guards,
incomplete scope, archive limits, 600 randomized comparisons against the existing
dominance relation, interruption before/after publication, and a real one-worker
scheduler handoff. Its formerly missed rotation now requires the independently
verified supply bill. RefillCycleCompletionTest extends this to 3..8-edge loops
at every entry, 300 independent integer-ratio oracles, returned tools/coproducts,
craftable feed, exact counts beyond long, deep known-summary reuse, restrictions,
conditional previews and completion/publication interruptions. This adds engine
options; host selection/display of these options is separate.

Per-material boundary diagnostics
--------------------------------

GraphPlan.missingBoundary(key) explains a displayed deficit without reclassifying
the order's feasibility. A nonmissing key returns null. Its source field is one of:

- TERMINAL_UNDER_CURRENT_COVERAGE: no producer is indexed in this planning view.
- CYCLE_BOUNDARY: completed support analysis places the material in a cycle.
- EXCLUDED_SOURCES: all captured producers were excluded from this view.
- UNEXPANDED_SOURCE: captured producers exist, without a stronger established
  explanation. Returned-only outputs, conflicting physical bindings, strategy
  restrictions and finite escapes can fall here; no missing-path proof follows.
- UNASSESSED: optional diagnostics did not complete for this material.

The independent explicitFinalSeed flag reports a final obligation recorded in
plan.seeds(), even if preserveSeeds is false. It can coexist with any source
boundary. It does not assert that the whole deficit is seed stock, that the
player explicitly requested that seed, or that its amount is globally necessary.
Cycles are support facts, not minimum startup quantities or complete cycle
coverage. Finite-ranked alternatives need not receive cycle analysis.

Terminal is scoped to the supplied catalog, including in a conditional preview.
It never asserts complete host capture, absence of omitted variants, or necessity
across alternative bills. Captured publication and metadata-only copies retain
these facts without changing coverage or seed optimality. Rebuilt execution/bill
builders clear old facts; unannotated results, including some recovered stateful
refills, remain UNASSESSED. Consumers must handle that value.

Annotation follows all refill quality passes and borrows the completed rank
analysis. It only checks existing source-index/rank facts, without scanning all
producers or retaining the compiler/graph in the published plan. No-prefix
terminal bills share a constant default. Other bills use at most 2048 diagnostic
visits per request, across views/workers, in slices of 64. This allowance is
separate from solving, compilation and cache retention, but advances the worker
clock for fairness. PlanningBudget.refillDiagnosticWork() exposes its usage.
Optional memory refusal/time limits keep the completed bill and proof; unvisited
keys stay UNASSESSED. Cancellation still propagates. Annotation shares the plan's
immutable execution/maps instead of copying a large witness.

RefillBoundaryTest covers source/input permutations, actual main/fallback and
complete/incomplete captured publication, returned tools, binding conflicts,
final seeds, copy isolation, memory/time/cancellation, a request-wide concurrent
cap, independent budget accounts, and real one-worker scheduler handoffs. Existing
continuation tests check that annotation retains the unchanged witness/maps.
This is additive engine diagnostic metadata; host UI display is separate.

Ordinary fallback under a tight request budget
---------------------------------------------

The initial funded search still runs first. Its positive witnesses and the
separate witnessByCost entry keep their old path. If funded search fails,
the forward material tree can repeat a shared dependency thousands of times
before finishing its first summary, second verification fold and count pass.
Backward refill cannot repair a tree that has already spent the whole budget.

Expansion tracks a reference lower bound for the original unshared pipeline.
Ordinary batches require both port scans and the count traversal. Returned-input
batches additionally require separate key scaling and parent merging; compact
long-overflow repeats still contain at least one fresh positive batch per emitted
step. If that bound reaches a quarter of the remaining work, the first fold also
records calling-thread work. Small previews avoid enabling per-charge thread
accounting just for this optional measurement. Once that fold completes, it supplies
a stronger reference bound for the identical second fold, including its caller
checkpoints. These describe the unshared pipeline; they are not lower bounds for
the accelerated path below. Work performed by another budget-sharing worker
cannot inflate the measured fold.

Only when that reference bound exceeds the remaining request work does fallback
discard the unfinished tree. It then starts backward expansion directly from
delivery plus explicit final seeds. The empty word is funded by those goals;
no synthetic recipe or forward loan supplies production. Forced requests add
an explicit net-production floor, so a supplied target startup seed cannot
count toward newly crafted output. The complete resulting word is verified
against its exact starting supplies, target and final seeds before publication.
The original remaining budget is used; there is no fresh allowance or retry
after exhaustion. All abandoned tree workspace is released first.

Completed material trees with at least 256 emitted steps reuse their already
computed immutable summary in the first funded verifier. This verifier still
checks every prefix requirement, delivery and final seed. Its caller only reads
the checked summary, so it omits the otherwise unused gross production/count
pass. Funded search witnesses, witnessByCost, public verification and the existing
force/source guards retain their previous behavior. Small material trees retain
their previous work-scaled refinement scheduling too.

The same completed word can then lend its summary to backward expansion. The
borrow requires the exact original plan object, and prefix assembly preserves
its recipe bindings, rejecting conflicting IDs. Summary lookup uses word identity;
equal but different words cannot reuse another binding's arithmetic. The new
prefix is folded normally, then the complete word's prefix requirements, target
and seeds are verified against its new supplies. This is local immutable suffix
reuse, with no stock/recipe cache across requests.

Retained vectors require an optional reservation of at most one sixteenth of the
available workspace. Refusal uses ordinary folding; completion, limits and
cancellation release the lease. At publication, reuse is also declined unless
mandatory preparation has already reached the 2048-work quality-scheduling
floor. This avoids shrinking later work-scaled quality allowances for small
refinements. The old unshared routing reference is deliberately retained so the
optimization does not divert already successful compact-goal previews.

FallbackCompactRefillTest covers repeated shared dependencies through 20 levels,
catalog/input reversal, returned tools, explicit seeds, existing target stock,
forced production, and leaf totals beyond long. Small words receive independent
primitive replay; large words have exact conservation and prefix checks. It
checks the admission bound against actual full folds/count verification and
exercises work/memory/cancellation exits. Existing completed high-budget results
are compared separately with the pre-change engine.

FallbackSummaryReuseTest covers large repeated suffixes, returned tools, counts
beyond long, changed goals/seeds/prefixes and binding identity, ordinary versus
summary-only verification, optional reservation refusal, work/time/cancellation,
and scheduler ownership transfer. Small refinements preserve their old work
counts; independently replayed larger words preserve primitive input enabling,
target delivery and final seeds. Missing metadata cannot be read as force credit.

In two archived fallback requests, the original 262144-work limit now completes
at 202714/197045 work. Their 131072 successes and generous-budget words/bills are
unchanged. A paired sweep of 94 case/cap combinations from 131072 through 393216,
plus 1048576, completes all sampled requests: 32 formerly stopped, 62 already
complete and unchanged. Accounted peaks do not increase in these samples.

This admission rule is deliberately conservative. It does not estimate every
later source-ranking/expansion cost. The sampled discontinuities are fixed, but
this does not establish general monotonic success for every possible work cap.
Other trees can still expire in later refinement. The replacement bill may
choose different incomparable supplies from an
old run given a larger budget. It is a verified refill witness, not a minimum
supply certificate or a proof of infeasibility under the original stock.
