Missing material expansion from verified execution requirements
==============================================================

A valid refill witness proves that its listed supplies fund an execution. It
does not prove that every listed supply is a terminal material. The former
forward preview could stop at a depth/alternative limit, or borrow an earlier
input after a later sibling consumed it. Verification accepted those loans
because they funded the word, leaving craftable intermediates in the UI.

MissingMaterialExpansion refines the selected, verified witness in both main
planning and ordinary fallback. Its prefix phase preserves the execution suffix,
recipe bindings and final seed obligations. For a prepended batch of n firings,
each resource's new requirement is exactly

  max(input + max(0, input - output) * (n - 1), old_need - net_output * n).

This sparse update retains coproduct credits, returned tools and ordering. It
does not recursively copy the whole suffix. Counts remain BigInteger values;
large batches use the existing compact PlanStep representation. A forced-order
prefix must also replace any finished-target units it consumes, so existing
target stock cannot replace the suffix's verified production credit.

RefillProducerOrder indexes only the deficit's reachable producer support.
Finite derivations from terminal supplies receive increasing ranks. Iterative
SCC analysis ranks a remaining component above its outside feedstocks, with
the same rank for all members of a cycle. An accepted prefix must reduce the
selected deficit and can increase only strictly lower-ranked deficits. This
prevents alternating loans around a cycle. A real startup seed can remain;
an ordinary item downstream of that cycle is expanded to the actual seed.
An available raw escape route is expanded even if another producer cycles.
Productive cycles may reduce seed loans while expanding their feedstocks.

The rank index factors each reached recipe's input support once. Material
nodes keep their original producer lists, while a shared recipe AND node
owns readiness and reached positive-net outputs. A p-output/q-input recipe
therefore retains O(p+q) incidence instead of copying q inputs p times. It
does not discover unrelated coproducts or erase a returned tool/self-input
enablement requirement. External supplies and excluded sources keep their
existing boundaries.

Completed sources merge later coproduct outputs by original incidence order.
This preserves material ranks even when first-ready finite derivations depend
on queue order. Single-output sources emit directly without a merge queue.
An unshared sole producer lends its input list directly to material SCC
traversal, avoiding an extra vertex on ordinary chains and simple cycles.
SCC analysis traverses the factored graph: recipe-only components add zero
material depth, and a component containing materials adds one. Cyclic members
share a rank above outside feedstocks, as in the projected material graph.

Discovery, readiness propagation, DFS, component extraction, outside-depth
inspection and rank publication use saved cursors. A rank step visits at most
64 operations before returning to its caller. This is a cooperative work
bound, not a wall-time guarantee against GC, host key operations or scheduling.
The extra recipe-vertex state has a cost for narrow single-output catalogs;
wide joint producers are the intended memory/work improvement. A completed
rank index keeps no inventory-dependent or cross-request mutable state.

For a growth producer with input a, available stock s, current requirement R
and positive net output d, the old ceil(deficit/d) count can run past the
startup requirement plateau. Expansion also evaluates the exact breakpoint

  max(1, ceil(max(0, R - max(a, s)) / d)).

The old count stays available: its extra coproducts or forced-target credit
can matter. Candidates are compared by their entire before-requirement
vectors, including unchanged resources, and by forced-target production
credit. A strictly componentwise better candidate replaces the selected one;
incomparable candidates keep catalog priority. The existing preference for
a fully stock-funded producer remains. No item/fluid exchange rate or sum
of unlike missing quantities is used. Candidate scratch memory is charged
and released even when comparison or evaluation exhausts a budget.

RefillWordPruning optionally reduces excessive counts already in the completed
word. It works backward from delivery and final seeds, tracking inventory at
each boundary of the ORIGINAL prefix. Each positive net output d requires at
least ceil(max(0, shortened_suffix_need - prefix_inventory) / d) executions.
The maximum of these bounds, followed by exact backward prefix requirements,
preserves a legal word within the original initial-supply vector. Shrinking a
nonpositive-net resource's count cannot worsen its startup/total requirement.
This goal-aware rewrite may discard unrequested final surplus; the generic
PlanFlowPruning substitution still preserves every original net output because
it can operate inside an unknown surrounding word.

The original bound recipes and order stay fixed. Useful coproducts and final
seeds enter the backward need, including seeds not touched by any recipe. Forced
orders retain their previous net target credit; orders needing a separate
indivisible-count production proof are left unchanged. Consumed configuration
inputs also decline rewriting, preserving their per-push contract. Sequences
use explicit cursors; composite repeat bodies stay opaque and their internal
order is never flattened into independent batches. Nested exact counts larger
than long remain compact. Self-returning recipes trigger this pass; a catalog
with only multi-recipe cycles and no direct input returns is not yet selected.

Both initial withdrawal and missing supply must be componentwise no larger.
A separate complete prefix/delivery/seed verification precedes publication.
When the prefix pass just verified this exact immutable word and binding tuple,
its completed summary is borrowed within the same request. The owning verifier
keeps its memory charge until the child closes; unrelated words and terminal-only
bills use their own fold. This avoids repeating the original-word verification.
The old verified bill remains available during refinement and on work/time/
memory exits; cancellation propagates. Scratch memory is charged and released.
The work cap is at most 8192 units and one quarter of remaining work (with a
single bounded continuation of overshoot), scaled down for small expansions.
Plain cursor slices visit at most 64 entries; child folds/verifiers own their
slices. Terminal-only missing bills can enter this optional pass too, without
rebuilding the producer graph or losing the bill at an exhausted entry boundary.
Ordinary recipes with no direct returns decline before folding the whole word.

Mixed-source stock allocation is an optional improvement of the completed bill.
While inspecting ordinary candidates, expansion detects positive partial batches
that introduce no additional deficits. Only if such a hint remains useful after
whole-source selection does a second bounded pass try these batch counts. Every
whole-source candidate is still considered before taking a partial source, so a
later wholly funded producer keeps its priority.

For an input with amount a, net loss l > 0, suffix requirement R and allowable
initial ceiling C = stock + existing deficit, a no-added-deficit batch must obey

  n <= 1 + floor((C - a) / l), and n <= floor((C - R) / l).

If a > C there is no positive funded batch. Returned inputs with zero net loss
still require their startup amount. External inputs omit the inventory bound;
forced-target consumption additionally respects the existing production credit.
The minimum of these exact bounds is evaluated again through the normal complete
support/rank checks. All counts remain BigInteger, including amounts beyond long.
Input scanning is resumable and does not enumerate firings or durability states.
The alternative borrows the completed producer ranking and source lists from its
parent: compiler, missing roots, external supplies and exclusions are identical,
and no ranking data is mutated. The parent alone owns and releases that workspace.
This avoids repeating graph discovery/SCC work for the optional search.

Partial funding alone is not a quality guarantee: a greedy choice can consume
stock needed by another branch, or lose a useful coproduct. Therefore the ordinary
expanded, verified plan is retained before attempting any mixture. The alternative
starts from the same original suffix, stock, exclusions and seed obligations; its
complete word is verified before comparing the final missing-material vectors.
It is accepted only when every missing quantity is no larger and at least one is
smaller. Equal or incomparable bills retain the original exact plan. This compares
new supplies, not an invented material price or equal initial-stock consumption.

The optional pass uses the remaining request budget after ordinary expansion. Its
local work allowance is the minimum of one quarter of remaining work, 8192 units,
and max(512, four times ordinary expansion work); a continuation can overshoot that
soft allowance by at most 64 operations. Fewer than 512 remaining units skip it.
It shares request time/cancellation/memory limits and never changes configured
main/fallback/cache allowances. The verified ordinary plan is available to an
owner interrupted during improvement. Work/time/memory exits retain that plan;
cancellation still propagates. The opportunity gate and cap limit extra work but
do not make this quality improvement free, or guarantee all possible mixtures.

Refill preparation, source selection, input/output support evaluation, full-vector
comparison, applying a selected prefix, reversing prefixes and building deficits
also retain cursors. Each expansion step performs at most 64 operations, or calls
one child rank/verification continuation on its own. A proposal remains private
until its support and comparison are complete; no next source sees a partially
applied requirement vector. Both retained proposals are released on interruption.
Iterator handoffs share their last port's work unit rather than charging another
arithmetic operation. Duplicate input/output support needs no temporary union set.

Publication transfers the completed private maps and flat step list into immutable
views, without copying the full vectors again for the funded verification plan.
These builders must never be mutated after transfer, including on close or another
step call. Public GraphPlan and PlanStep.Sequence constructors still defensively
copy and validate caller-owned collections. Public signatures are unchanged.

After expansion, PlanVerification checks the funded complete word again. A
work/time/memory exit does not publish a partially expanded bill; an already
completed ordinary bill remains available during optional improvement. Existing
main and fallback budgets remain independent and unchanged. Feasible plans
skip this pass. Incomplete capture stays conditional: expansion does not prove
missing recipe/state domains infeasible or claim globally minimal supplies.
Changed plans drop stale seed-optimality and alternative-plan metadata.

Final verification streams material keys from the completed exact summary, then
the target and independent seeds, in the same order as before. It no longer copies
a large union or rescans irrelevant initial inventory. The exact fold retains a
delta entry for every prefix requirement, including zero-net returned tools;
initial keys outside that set and outside the goals have zero need/change/goal
and their quantities were already validated as nonnegative. Up to 32 key visits
share one continuation, without an extra arithmetic charge for a yield boundary.

GraphRecipe records input/output overlap once at construction on the smaller port
side, combining the old input/configuration/reusable aggregation loops. Ordinary
batch folding reads this immutable fact instead of scanning recipe ports at every
batch boundary. Logical reusable returns still take the general fold, even when
they do not represent physical outputs. Construction remains linear in recipe
size; this does not introduce an inventory-dependent or cross-request cache.

Completed summary/count traversals relinquish their temporary indices without a
bulk clear loop. Only positive batches under positive repeat edges enter counting,
so every indexed recipe gets a positive propagated count and needs no final zero
filter. Zero batches/repeats, shared words and exact counts beyond long remain
covered by independent literal-count tests.

An interrupted preview must not erase an already completed original-domain
infeasibility proof. GraphPlanningWork retains that proof with an unavailable
preview diagnostic; otherwise the actual work/time/memory limit remains the
result. A fully verified expansion completed before its parent's publication
boundary is also retained. Capture and request owners still prevent a local
proof from escaping an incomplete or restricted recipe domain, including in
diagnostic text. Cancellation remains exceptional, not an infeasibility result.

MissingMaterialExpansionTest independently replays primitive operations for
shared prerequisites, returned tools, coproducts, finite escapes and cycles,
5000-level chains, wide fan-in and randomized DAGs. It also checks forced-order
credit, counts beyond long and interrupted-work cleanup. GraphFallbackTest
retains exact stock, force-crafting, cycle, configuration and budget controls.
The local player-world and historical-dump comparisons stay outside Git.

RefillQualityTest covers growth plateaus with stock and final seeds, joint
production under reversed input/source order, incomparable supplies, useful
coproducts, target credit and temporary-workspace cleanup. Its arithmetic
quantity oracle and primitive replay are independent of candidate selection.
RefillMixtureTest checks 500 independent multi-source allocation oracles, reversed
source order, returned-input startup, shared stock, final seeds, target credit,
excluded sources and exact counts beyond long. It retains the old whole bill when
a locally funded coproduct choice makes the final supplies worse. Real scheduler
handoffs and work/time/memory/cancellation exits cover the optional continuation.
RefillWordPruningTest adds the complete main/fallback growth examples, retained
seeds and useful coproducts, forced credit, opaque/shared/zero/large repeat words,
physical bindings, incomplete scope, external supplies and configuration tools.
Its 500 randomized words use independent literal prefix requirements and replay;
every accepted initial/missing vector is compared componentwise with the original.
Limits at continuation boundaries and a real one-worker handoff cover retention,
cleanup, cancellation and cooperative scheduling.
MissingExpansionLimitTest observes real production state-machine transitions
to expansion, then interrupts work/time/memory or cancellation. It exercises
both completed and absent root proofs, completed child publication, ordinary
request ownership, and complete/incomplete captures without injecting proofs.

RefillProducerOrderTest checks material SCCs using independent boolean
derivability and transitive closure, preserves incidence-sensitive finite
ranks, and enforces a linear budget on wide joint producers. It also covers
external/excluded/raw-escape boundaries, self-input growth and returned tools,
interruption during rank/SCC cursors, and a real one-worker scheduler where a
small queued request must run within one bounded rank slice of a large order.

RefillContinuationTest exercises 12000 sources, 4096-port comparisons, 4096-step
prefix publication, primitive witness replay and public collection isolation.
It cancels every observed continuation boundary, checks work/memory limit cleanup,
and queues small requests on the actual one-worker scheduler during source scans,
support evaluation, dominance comparison, application and publication preparation.

VerificationContinuationTest measures actual material hash/equality visits as well
as charged work, including the summary-to-verifier handoff. It exercises wide
outputs, independent seeds, unused initial inventory, late missing seeds, recipe
classification, shared/zero-count words, cancellation/time limits and real
one-worker scheduler handoffs. SummaryBatchContinuationTest additionally checks
that every literal prefix deficit remains in the verification key domain.

Known boundaries after the refill review at 224e9172:

- Optional verified whole-word reduction now removes dominated firings already
  selected upstream, including the complete main-solver growth example (1001
  firings becomes one when no final A seed is required). It remains bounded and
  conservative: it does not replace recipes, optimize inside opaque composite
  repeat bodies, rewrite consumed configuration pushes, or claim global minimum
  supplies. It retains existing forced-production credit even if it is surplus.
- A bounded alternative pass covers stock-funded producer mixtures that the
  single-source proposals miss, with whole-bill dominance guarding acceptance.
  Its hints and greedy allocation are incomplete. Same-rank multi-step cycles
  can remain seed boundaries. A stopped expansion is not proof that a material has no useful
  producer, nor a certificate of minimum supply.
- Refill enumeration/publication and final verifier key traversal use bounded
  continuations; verifier union construction and repeated recipe-disjointness
  scans are eliminated. Recipe construction remains linear. Host key/recipe
  operations, collection growth, big-integer arithmetic, composite known-summary
  lookups and GC have no hard wall-time guarantee. This is not a claim that every
  operation in the entire planning pipeline has bounded latency.
- Public per-material boundary reasons are not introduced by this change.
