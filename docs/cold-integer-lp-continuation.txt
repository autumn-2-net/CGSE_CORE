Cold integer-LP continuation (2026-10-11)

Scope
-----
This is a numerical state-lifecycle improvement, not a new integer solving
algorithm or an infeasibility proof. Existing Boolean LP and integer models
with at most 128 ambient variables keep their previous numerical path. The
larger bounded-integer support channel explicitly opts into cold continuation.
Its complete-row 128-column projection, exact cut reconstruction, original
row/domain verification and request budget remain unchanged.

The cold simplex phase, artificial-variable cleanup cursor and per-phase pivot
limit now survive local visits. A local allowance may pause only after a full
pivot commits its tableau and basis swap. Work beyond the local allowance is
at most that pivot and is still charged to the shared request. Cancellation,
time and global work checks continue inside scalar batches; such terminal
interruptions discard the whole tableau, including a half-written pivot.

Resuming a partial cold tableau requires the same ordered coefficients,
objective and RHS. A completed feasible basis retains the existing separately
validated warm-start behavior. No partial basis is parked in the two-basis
cache. The LCG owner resumes a pending cold attempt before changing branch
bounds; no numerical status or point bypasses integer/execution verification.

Cold continuation also owns its completed integer projection while pending.
Its source rows must retain identity and both bound arrays are snapshotted and
compared by value; the projected immutable row list can then be matched without
rescanning all numerical rows on every tiny visit. A completed numerical
proposal is held until coordinate lifting and exact cut reconstruction finish.
If interpretation needs a larger uninterrupted visit, the owner accrues
already-granted local work before retrying; the shared request cap is unchanged.
A failed maximum-sized interpretation or optional memory refusal discards this
state, allowing normal search to continue without a fabricated conclusion.
No projection or unfinished result crosses request ownership.

Official references consulted
-----------------------------
https://github.com/google/or-tools/blob/stable/ortools/glop/revised_simplex.cc
GLOP's primal/dual iteration loops check deterministic limits at iteration
boundaries; SaveState records basis statuses and its error path marks the
state for revalidation. This informed safe continuation ownership and phase
boundaries. CGSE's implementation is independent, uses its existing tableau,
and does not import GLOP's revised-simplex factorization or pricing algorithms.

https://www.scipopt.org/doc/html/heur__feaspump_8c_source.php
SCIP's feasibility-pump implementation was inspected as a possible integer
proposal tool. A local prototype alternating LP distance projection and integer
rounding did not solve these four equality models (three seeds each, 120 LP
rounds); it is not included in production. This negative experiment must not be
described as importing SCIP's algorithm or matching SCIP's implementation.

Evidence and limits
-------------------
The generated sparse-equality corpus contains four residual models (144/257
variables, two seeds) in three complete-corpus orders. Under the original
20,000,000 work request limit all 12 remain UNRESOLVED. The 257-variable models
also have incomplete local LP support; cold continuation alone cannot replace
missing integer propagation/search machinery. UNRESOLVED is not UNSAT.
The 822-model comparison uses one worker; its unresolved original frontier
does not trigger the width > 1 early root portfolio. The measured cold LP gain
is retained-channel evidence, not a demonstrated speedup of those 12 main-path
cases.

For the unpermuted 144-variable models, eight 65,536-work local LP visits:
  n144-s2: old 516,189 work, 8 builds, 0 proposals;
           new 219,849 work, 1 build, 6 proposals (5 warm hits).
  n144-s1: old 516,187 work, 8 builds, 0 proposals;
           new 204,731 work, 1 build, 6 proposals (5 warm hits).
These are continuous proposals, not valid rounded integer witnesses. The
complete 144-column LP also progresses across visits; the full cold solve and
the sliced solve return the same numeric trajectory. Full 257-column cold
relaxation still needs more than 1,000,000 work for its first proposal.

Final integrated production comparison against freshly compiled 2360b7f2,
with the same 822 count-model inputs and complete-corpus permutations,
no wall-clock cutoff, 20M work and 256 MiB:

ColdLpContinuationTest checks 24 full/sliced numeric trajectories bit-for-bit
(203 resumptions), one-pivot local overshoot, worker migration, completed-basis
warm starts, RHS/matrix/order/objective invalidation, in-pivot cancellation,
global work cutoff and memory disposal. Two distinct single-thread executors
alternate every pending visit. A 144-variable projected kernel and its actual
LCG owner must produce an LP suggestion with continued 4096-work visits; the
owner additionally exercises an interpretation grant larger than that quantum.
Maximum 128-column/512-input-row supports include a phase-I contradiction;
returned cuts are reconstructed independently from their original parents.
When the request's remaining LP share drops below a pending interpretation's
requirement, the optional proposal is abandoned so ordinary search can proceed.
Earlier pending-tableau-only code rebuilt the projection on each visit and
stalled on this scenario, so tableau retention alone was insufficient.
Existing RationalLpLearningTest,
IntegerLpLearningTest, IntegerLpProposalTest and IntegerLpPortfolioTest also
pass: exact integer witnesses and independent proof replay remain mandatory.
ColdLpPhaseOneTest independently checks 48 contradictory models with shuffled
rows, alternating workers and 258 resumptions. Their phase-I duals match the
uninterrupted numerical reference exactly, and infeasible tableaus are released
immediately. Numerical infeasibility still does not constitute an integer proof.
Local raw inputs, probes, logs and baseline comparisons are kept outside Git.
