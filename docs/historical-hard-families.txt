3. Integer equalities and source/fuel allocation: distinct algebraic services

   The four equality models need integer-lattice/box reasoning, not Boolean
   packing neighborhoods. The two source/fuel models have vector allocation
   structure and exact residual checks. Their next common improvements should
   be selected from their own runtime failures: reduced integral coordinates,
   residual completion and structural dispatch between those services. Do not
   interpret the size of the Boolean backlog as evidence for applying its
   algorithm to these models. Their latest results are inherited from the
   earlier family campaigns; this increment does not freshly solve all six.

   A subsequent sparse-equality campaign confirms the four equality limits at
   40M on 720a8141. Their retained LCG searches learn many conflicts but do not
   converge. A bounded companion now combines exact short-row sum supports
   with integer conflict learning, starting from the compiler view's certified
   root bounds. Original search frontiers remain available. It does not expand
   a high-dimensional dense lattice or export its private negative conclusions.
   See sparse-equality-support.txt. The four canonical limits remain UNKNOWN;
   the new companion is retained for verified complementary capability and
   bounded costs, not advertised as four newly solved canonical cases.

   The next priority within this group is n144-s1: earlier complete-model SCIP
   runs returned independently verified integer witnesses. Shorter native
   timeouts do not revoke that evidence. The other three remain lower priority
   unless a verified reference witness or a shared structural improvement
   provides a more useful comparison; see retained-finite-hints.txt.

4. Refill quality, unsupported conversion and unconfirmed reports

   The refill group already had executable, verified bills. Runtime tracing
   exposed repeated material folds through singleton wrappers, independently
   rebuilt equal spans, and final verification that did not borrow the pruning
   pass's already owned unit summary. Exact identity peeling and span sharing
   now remove those costs without unrolling loops or moving intervening steps.
   A completed grouping also borrows its original verifier by identity/lifetime,
   rather than waiting for an elapsed-work threshold. Short-window analysis and
   saturated-quality cursors omit empty handoffs while still charging traversal.

   The same 1536 inputs improve 1462 -> 1536 scoped-bound results at 8192 work;
   total quality work falls 5,696,230 -> 4,188,094, peak reservation 201,056 ->
   136,608 bytes. Every prior bound result has identical supplies/counts, with no
   per-case work or peak-reservation increase. These are headless quality-pass
   measurements, not live-host latency or a global minimum-supply proof. The
   74 recovered variants are mandatory in RefillNormalizedWordTest. Arbitrary
   longer words and very small allowances remain bounded and incomplete.

   Unsupported conversion needs a representation
   change; reports without sufficient input need reproducible evidence. Neither
   should inflate the numerical solver backlog or acquire an invented oracle.

Prioritization rule: group by observed lost progress, representation boundary,
search/verification phase and structural coordinates. Compare a whole failing
family plus its successful controls before accepting a common change. Keep
original inputs, budgets, orderings and all unsuccessful experiments locally;
promote verified recovered cases into mandatory repository regression coverage.
