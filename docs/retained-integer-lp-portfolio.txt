Retained bounded-integer LP in the root portfolio (2026-10-11)
===========================================================

Boundary and diagnosis
----------------------
Against 19afbe9b, the maintained main-count corpus had 14 undecided orders:
four large sparse-equality models in three orders, plus wide-79 orders 1/2.
Direct bounded-integer LP/LCG could solve wide-79 order 1. The main pipeline
did not give that combination a comparable retained search: its view scout
stopped at 16K work, while this model's first numerical proposal needed about
39K. Merely enlarging the scout or admitting another short-sliced view arm
did not solve the two orders. Interrupted cold numerical pivots were discarded.

The root auxiliary portfolio now offers an additional integer-LP/LCG arm with
the existing 131072-work retained LCG slices and 262144-work owner visits.
It retains its bound trail, clauses, activities, cuts and completed numerical
bases. All work and memory remain charged to the same request. There is no
larger request cap, fresh budget per visit, or fallback-tree modification.

Admission uses a bounded scan for a complete finite integer row support with
a nonunit coefficient. Boolean-only, fixed-only, unbounded-boundary and small
remaining-budget inputs do not enter the extra LP search. An unrelated outer
unbounded column does not exclude a complete finite local support. No omitted
term is assigned zero. The existing local LP independently checks its limits.

Keep both decision orders
-------------------------
The ordinary minimized-conflict LCG remains active alongside the LP variant.
Replacing it solved wide-79 but lost wide-52 order 2. Auxiliary deduplication
therefore checks whether learned relaxation is enabled in addition to exact
model/domain identity. Removing that distinction loses wide-52 order 2 again
at the same 20M cap. These are complementary strategies, not identical work.

The extra arm has the same explicit root scope as the existing witness arms.
The proof task keeps its separate goal. A local pause, numerical infeasibility,
admission refusal or exhausted allowance cannot close the primary search.
Only exact row combinations and independently checked integer witnesses enter
the existing proof/execution validation path. No numerical status is promoted
to an exact negative conclusion.

Validation at an unchanged request cap
-------------------------------------

request, 256 MiB, one worker, no wall deadline:

                       Baseline      Candidate
  Conclusive              790            792
  Undecided                14             12

No previously conclusive case is lost. wide-79 order 1 now uses 14,759,981
work and order 2 uses 5,283,105; both independently validate in original count
coordinates and as primitive recipe execution. All three wide-52 orders stay
conclusive. The gained orders and the replacement/deduplication counterexample
are maintained in tests/regression/count-boundaries.json.

There is a measured cost tradeoff. Work on the 790 common conclusive cases
increases from 599,039,561 to 604,524,003 (about 0.9%). wide-52 orders 1 and 2
increase from 9,690,287 / 6,570,839 to 14,128,865 / 10,817,238. This adds
coverage at the same cap; it is not a claim of uniform speed improvement.

The standard suite passes 96 programs/batches, including the updated maintained
count boundaries. All 244 graph fixtures pass in three permutations (732).
IntegerLpPortfolioTest independently enumerates 180 finite models (123 SAT,
57 UNSAT), verifies their certificates, exercises 10 learned cuts, cross-worker
continuation, 240 cancellation points and eight work/memory limit combinations.
Its final strengthened safety run accompanies the standard campaign. The
existing RootPortfolioTest also covers concurrent requests with and without
parallel budget expansion. Offline assemble and source-JAR equality pass.

Remaining limits
----------------
The 12 undecided orders are the 144/257-variable sparse-equality s1/s2 models;
this change does not prove them infeasible. A cold numerical tableau interrupted
inside a pivot is still discarded, not resumed from partial mutation. The
retained root arm provides usable slices and reuses completed numerical bases;
arbitrary partial-pivot continuation is a separate, unimplemented extension.
This campaign validates standalone CGSE, not a live Minecraft/AE server.
