Review follow-up: continuation scheduling and exact LP proposals
===============================================================
2026-10-09, baseline c67ead23.

Both supplied reviews describe older snapshots (15a473de and e788d3d3).
The earlier six priorities are substantially wired in: CountStride reaches
eligible separator/MITM scouts; candidate outcomes feed retained searches;
CountHall reuses a covering matching and alternating SCC/free-resource paths;
GraphCatalogIndex and GraphDemandProgram share catalog/topology work;
CountScheduleOrder/CountStartup provide execution hints and scoped explanations;
CountViewCuts and affine proof records validate cross-coordinate transfers.
These are bounded mechanisms, not complete arbitrary-domain or catalog solvers.

This change fixes two remaining gaps in those mechanisms.

1. A retained branch returned to the ordinary pending queue after one OPEN
   worker quantum, even when its current continuation visit was unfinished.
   Growing fresh child queues could therefore bury already-paid root searches.
   A deferred visit now stays in the continuation queue across its quanta.
   The existing quarter-share is measured using completed branch work instead
   of one out of four dispatch rounds: atomic compilation/LP steps can cost
   more than a quantum. The counters saturate and the comparison uses division,
   avoiding overflow from multiplying large configured work limits.

   This changes scheduling, not domain coverage. Children still cover a split
   parent's space; a parent continuation does not create duplicate children.
   Local cutoffs remain UNKNOWN. Root proof promotion and final execution
   verification keep their existing scope checks. No budget cap is increased.

2. CountLpLearning previously proposed row-sum multipliers by rounding scaled
   floating-point duals. That can turn simple rational proportions into large
   inexact integer ratios and lose useful exact cancellation/rounding strength.
   It now first tries bounded continued-fraction proposals, clears their common
   denominator and builds the same exact nonnegative original-row combination.
   Coefficients and the rounded right-hand side are computed with BigInteger.
   The old scaled proposals remain available when this optional attempt fails.

   A reconstructed multiplier is a heuristic suggestion, not a certified LP
   dual. No floating-point verdict becomes a proof. Every admitted consequence
   has explicit integer parents and a divisor; branch bounds only choose useful
   consequences and never become hidden premises. Reconstruction, arithmetic
   and memory use the existing request account and ownership boundary.

Remaining gaps, including a newly reproduced catalog boundary
-------------------------------------------------------------
1. Twenty original-order count models still exhaust the fixed budget: 13 known
   SAT and seven known UNSAT. They comprise seven lseu-1119 representations,
   six large sparse equalities, five subset sums and two wide-domain models.
   The 48-coordinate numeric admission and bounded MITM table limits remain.
   Stronger search/proof coverage is still needed; completed continuation work
   alone does not make the portfolio as capable as general SCIP/CP-SAT.

2. The full model still admits at most 8,192 recipes/resources and optional
   support neighborhoods at most 512 recipes (256 per contributed candidate).
   This review now has a concrete large-catalog failure: the complete 5,903
   manual + 62,284 virtual JEI recipes, requesting miracle_crystal at 1 or 1,000.
   Manual and manual-first orders verify; extra-first and seed-73 random orders
   reach the 20M search cap. An identical baseline replay has the same results
   AND charged work. This change does not introduce that failure. At 40M both
   versions still match and reach SEARCH_LIMIT on the four mixed-order cases;
   this is not a proof of infeasibility.
   The manual witness remains available in the larger catalog, so these are
   known feasible requests. Partial diagnostics show repeated small cyclic
   source choices, region model admission limits and a full 4,096-choice queue.
   They do not yet isolate one sufficient algorithmic fix.

   Reproduce the deliberately failing known-SAT check:
     python tests/datasets/run.py --java-home JDK17 --target gtlcore:miracle_crystal
       --amounts 1,1000 --modes extra-first,random --seed 73
       --milliseconds 0 --work 20000000 --require-feasible
   It exits nonzero on the search cutoff; do not count it as a passed regression.
   Increasing limits or calling a restricted model's failure globally UNSAT is
   not a fix. The next compiler/selection change should target this actual family.

3. Preparation/restoration/seed/fallback costs still lack fully unified candidate
   attribution. The new effort counters concern branch scheduling only. They
   do not close audit J, or measure startup guard pruning benefit automatically.

4. Aggregate compiler reservation estimates are not live JVM heap/RSS or proof
   that obsolete host snapshots lose their last reference. This update has no
   new real-server/client validation. Complete captured-network replay remains
   offline evidence; the prior live integration record applies to its own build.
