CGSE 0.1.5 review follow-up
=========================
Review baseline: d540c900, 2026-10-20. Version remains 0.1.5.
The review combines source observations, isolated-class tests and research
suggestions. A suggested search gap is not an invalid-plan or global-UNSAT bug.

Confirmed and implemented: terminal comparison of source routes
---------------------------------------------------------------
The multihop example was reproduced against the released engine, through
MissingMaterialExpansion, GraphFallback and GraphPlanner:

  X -> A; Y -> A; 100 raw -> X; 1 raw -> Y; A -> G.

With empty stock and one G requested, all three entries returned a verified
100-raw refill in the first order, and a verified 1-raw refill in reverse order.
The latter is both executable and minimal for this particular input: every
source ultimately consumes positive raw. This is a refill-quality defect,
not an invalid execution or an erroneous infeasibility proof.

RefillSourceSearch runs after the incumbent's existing expansion, mixture,
word reduction and optional cyclic searches. It examines the incumbent's
bindings and material ports for alternate positive-net producers with multihop
support. At most eight source preferences are retained. A preference never
removes a source or becomes a learned mathematical constraint.

Each trial starts from the same final delivery and explicit seed obligations.
The verified original word supplies the forced-target production credit.
An exact backward expansion rebuilds a complete execution, sharing one completed
producer-rank index across the trials. Previously accepted preferences can
compose across different materials; each new complete bill is compared with
the latest incumbent, not with an obsolete original.

The first-ready rank of a cheap short derivation need not admit a deeper
alternative. Only these private trials may cross the old rank order once per
preferred material. Applying that preference consumes the exception; repeated
visits again obey the old rank guard. Each trial permits at most eight exceptions,
and neither the original rank index nor ordinary expansion is changed.
Asymmetric chain depths and closed-cycle alternatives are regression cases.

Every candidate must finish expansion and execution verification. It replaces
the primary only if its missing-supply vector is componentwise no larger and
strictly smaller in at least one key. Equal or incomparable supplies preserve
the previous primary. Quantities of unlike items or fluids are never summed
as a price. Using more already-owned stock is permitted for strictly smaller
new supply; this objective is not minimum total resource use.

The original physical meaning of every bound recipe ID is preserved.
Excluded sources and external supplies keep their scope. Returned tools,
useful coproducts, exact counts beyond long, and final seeds enter the complete
execution check. Trials preserve the original net target-production credit.
Words needing a separate low-net forced-crafting proof are declined. All
original bindings are checked before trials, including late bindings outside
the hint window; per-push consumed configurations decline rewriting. Newly
selected per-push sources are also declined.

No changes are made to already-feasible orders or existing cyclic alternative
archives. A single-batch missing plan can opt in when the target has alternative
producers. Newly reached missing boundaries use the completed trial rank index,
so the original terminal-only shortcut cannot mislabel a new cycle seed.
The pass produces no negative proof, completeness claim or seed-optimality
certificate. It does not invoke the count or cyclic solver from fallback.
Nested seed-manufacturing subrequests decline this new optional pass, preserving
the owner's later search opportunity and the old single-batch admission there.

Limits and retained results
--------------------------
The lifetime allowance is min(8192, remaining search work / 4), shared by
discovery, original verification, rank construction, all trials and comparisons.
Hint discovery is capped at min(1024, max(128, allowance / 4)) visits.
Ordinary cursors yield every 64 visits; nested verifiers keep their own bounded
continuations. A quota can overshoot by one child turn, as in the existing
optional refinements. Memory is charged and released, including builders of
accepted words that outlive their trial.

These are additional optional search costs after the old stages; the change
is not advertised as a general speedup or as free cache work. A work, memory or
time exit retains the last completely verified publication. Cancellation
continues to propagate. No preparation or retained-solver budget is enlarged.

Maintained checks and an additional historical improvement
----------------------------------------------------------
RefillSourceSearchTest exercises all three entries, source orders, chain
depths, stock/tool/coproduct interactions, composed source preferences, external
and excluded supplies, conflicting bindings, conditional coverage, forced
credit, final seeds, large counts, primitive randomized path oracles, cutoffs
before and after publication, and scheduler handoff.

RefillMixtureTest retains its previous intermediate-stage assertion: a smaller
sum of incomparable materials still cannot displace the old mixture bill
(raw1=6, raw3=22). The new complete-route pass then finds raw1=2. Primitive
execution independently verifies that improvement using the original stock.
The old assertion was not weakened to accept arbitrary new bills.

A separate paired run of the released and built engines covers 32 combinations
of depth 1/8/64/512, single/alternate sources, missing/funded stock, and main/
fallback entry. Primitive replay accepts every word. Five bills improve and
27 remain unchanged; all 16 funded cases have identical charged work. Extra
charged work in this sample is at most 2665. In the five-recipe review example,
expansion/fallback spend 184 additional work and main spends 207. These are
work-accounting comparisons, not a wall-clock benchmark or a general speedup.

  - Built-library source-choice test: 772 assertions PASS.
  - Java 17 assemble and artifact checks PASS; all 1478 baseline API entries

Implemented follow-up: reallocate existing source counts
--------------------------------------------------------
The paired depth-64 and depth-512 main words already contained both X and Y.
The unused-source filter therefore left 201 and 102 raw respectively, although
the complete all-Y word consumes only 3 raw in each case. RefillReallocationTest
now retains those inputs, source-order/quantity variants, and independent
primitive all-Y witnesses. These are refill-quality cases; their old words
were valid and their infeasibility status was not a false proof.

A second RefillSourceSearch continuation admits multiple physically compatible
bound producers of the same material. It runs after the existing unused-source
trials, whose queue, quota and publications are preserved. A physical binding
does not constrain a recipe to its old count. Each candidate reconstructs the
complete prefix from the same delivery/seeds and original net production credit.
The original scan also records whether multiple eligible producers were already
bound. Complete discovery with no such choice skips reallocation; newly added
bindings do not immediately repeat the just-completed unused-source trials.
Incomplete discovery stays inconclusive, including long binding scans.

Independent coordinate changes alone are insufficient. In a three-material
case, rebuilding one route resets the other two mixtures to more expensive
defaults, so every isolated change fails componentwise comparison. The new arm
enumerates a bounded product of source preferences: at most eight retained hints
and at most eighteen combinations. It does not add exclusions or learned cuts.
Complete word verification and componentwise missing-supply improvement still
guard every publication; equal or incomparable bills retain the previous word.
This permits some joint route/count changes, not arbitrary count optimization.

Complete producer support can be borrowed from the preceding source pass under
the same compiler, roots and exclusions. Its owner remains alive until all
borrowing children close. Incomplete support is never reused. The new arm has
one lifetime search allowance:
  min(remaining work / 4, min(65536, max(8192, 128 * bound recipe count))).
Its discovery is capped at min(8192, max(128, allowance / 4)). Original
verification, rank construction, all trials and comparisons share that quota.
The global request budget and the separate cache-preparation account do not
change. This is extra optional search for missing previews, not free reuse.
The upper quota can overshoot by one existing bounded child turn.

Already-feasible orders, nested seed subrequests and existing cycle frontiers
keep their previous admission. Returned tools, useful coproducts, shared stock,
physical binding conflicts, exclusions, final seeds, large exact counts,
conditional coverage and force-production credit have maintained checks.
Work/time/memory exits retain the last complete accepted word, including through
the parent continuation and optional boundary annotation. Cancellation propagates.

The 32-request comparison against 43611f38 retains every prior bill, improves
the two mixed main bills, and leaves charged work identical on 27 requests
(including all 16 funded requests). The two improvements cost an additional
5362 and 33916 work. Three depth-512 requests retain their bill and spend
2572..2613 extra discovery work. Reserved peak memory is unchanged on all 32.
These are charged-work/estimated-residency observations, not a wall-time or
heap benchmark. No general speedup or globally minimal refill claim follows.

Follow-up validation with four logical CPUs: 38 related maintained programs and
741 curated graph requests PASS. The actual built library passes all 757
RefillReallocationTest assertions and the preceding 772 source-choice assertions.
Java 17 assemble, artifact verification and 1478 baseline API entries PASS.
Regression, paired comparison and build share identical production/test source
hashes. This is targeted validation, not a rerun of the full historical corpus
or the native game host. Version remains 0.1.5.

Remaining review work
---------------------
Exact equality supports: CountEqualitySupport still returns interval hulls.
Interior unsupported values can be lost even when both endpoints survive.
A private small-domain mask arm needs its own rollback/reason-scope tests and
paired sparse-equality comparisons before integration.

Packing: retain the existing asymmetric exchanges and successful old samples.
Persistent LP/core experiments must account for preparation and service costs;
the previously unsuccessful warm-LP integration is not enabled by this change.

Vector allocation: cross-block residual interfaces remain separate research.
Candidate exhaustion cannot prove that every first-block choice is impossible.

Refill optimization: this bounded source-preference search is not exhaustive
joint path/count optimization or a complete Pareto archive. Finite hint coverage,
rank refusals and quota exits do not certify a global minimum. Existing cycle
frontiers are retained as-is rather than flattened into a new search.
Both unused and already-bound source choices now have bounded positive arms.
General mixtures needing intermediate firing counts, larger joint choices and
combinations outside the hint window remain incomplete. The long depth-512
fallback still retains its 300-raw incumbent at the first discovery/rank limit;
it contains only one bound source, so it is not admitted to bound reallocation.
This unchanged fallback is not reported as a recovered case.

Compiler feedback/modeling: newly added producer columns still require
recertifying bounds and cuts. No signature weakening, arbitrary state jumping,
instantaneous-machine-capacity bound, or independent-slot substitution for a
true tuple relation is introduced. Host integration and general temporal
entity assignment remain distinct from portable core regression evidence.
