Fixes found by the campaign
---------------------------
1. CountJump could select the integer immediately below a feasible breakpoint
   when two large negative gains rounded to the same double. Compare tied moves
   by subtracting their exact residual violations before conversion. This only
   changes candidate ranking, never proof arithmetic. The original JumpSweep
   reproducer and 643 maintained boundary/continuation cases pass.

2. Sparse certified cuts inherited the dense bound-sharing dimension limit.
   Separate sparse publication/import admission from dense bound admission;
   preserve view ownership, covering semantics, certification and the existing
   term/byte/work limits. Dense bounds still retain the 1024-variable cap.
   Proof logging still declines transfers outside independent replay admission.
   The original 1699-variable WideCut reproducer and maintained scope, proof,
   cancellation and memory-refusal checks through 8192 variables pass.

3. A forced target already present in stock admitted neutral turnover in the
   count model even though final candidate verification requires positive net
   target gain. This could enumerate invalid counts until the 20M search cap.
   Add the necessary gain >= 1 condition where the material balance does not
   already imply it. Preserve the per-push configuration-input relaxation and
   gross-production semantics; do not require net gain to equal the request.
   The original CycleReview reproducer now passes. Sixty ring/order/seed cases
   pass at 1M, with separate productive-startup, configuration, warm-scope and
   Long.MAX_VALUE checks. Model memory and work include the additional row.

The three targeted solver regressions each reject the corresponding pre-fix
implementation or restored defective admission rule in a local negative control.

Two test defects were also corrected. The independent fixture/corpus checker
used net gain >= request, incorrectly rejecting valid self-copying witnesses
that consume their target seed. It now separately counts primitive gross
production, positive net gain and final delivery; returned read-arc tokens are
not production. Five curated fixtures and FixtureProductionTest pin this.
Runtime unit tests assumed two provider dispatches always fit a real 2 ms tick
under concurrent load. They now use a controlled clock plus explicit deadline,
continuation and nanoTime-wrap checks. The public runtime clock and 2 ms limit
are unchanged.
