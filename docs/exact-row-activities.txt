Exact retained row activities, 2026-10-11
=======================================

Arithmetic and scope
--------------------
LCG already retained each row's exact minimum, but every endpoint event still
allocated arbitrary-precision products and sums. A row with signed-long root
endpoints, widths and coefficient magnitudes now stores its minimum as a long
when the sum also fits. Each product and addition is checked. Overflow promotes
the old exact total before applying the update with BigInteger; undo does not
silently demote it. Rebuilding an invalidated/sleeping row independently recomputes
the sum and can re-enter the primitive representation. Infinite or wider domains
continue through arbitrary-precision arithmetic. Reasons still refer to their
original frozen trail prefix, not the live cache.

PB's positive-weight rows use a separate, stronger admission condition: the
sum of ALL normalized weights must fit a nonnegative signed long. Every assigned
subset and undo is consequently bounded, including newly compiled learned rows.
The compiled capacity lies in [0, total weight), so its difference with the spent
weight also fits. Original offsets, arbitrary-precision explanation construction,
derived-row verification and final original-coordinate checks remain exact.
Checking only the current subtotal or capacity would not be sufficient admission.

The extra scalar fields fit the existing per-row reservation estimates and
replace frequently allocated totals. No per-event table, new search strategy,
request limit, operation weight or proof rule is added. Existing work charges
are retained, including those on updates now performed with primitive arithmetic.

Focused evidence
----------------
CountLcgActivityTest independently recomputes 116,650 original-row sums after
endpoint tightening/relaxation and 1,440 invalidation/rebuild events, including
signed boundaries, cancelling wide terms and infinity ownership. Explicit cases
exercise overflowing products, overflowing additions and undo after promotion.
The existing CountLcgFiniteTest pairs 256 exhaustive model trajectories with the
arbitrary-precision path, checking work, bounds, reasons and proof replay; its
630 individual row boundaries also remain covered.

CountCdclFiniteTest pairs 300 searches against forced BigInteger row execution.
The models are independently enumerated in original coordinates. Work, assigned
values, trail, learning, derived rows and final answers match at every step;
proofs replay independently. These runs include 24 resumptions, 190 conflicts
and 118 derived weighted rows. Huge offsets, weights on both sides of the long
boundary and fixed variables do not bypass admission. The established decision-
queue oracle additionally covers restarts, imports and resource cleanup.

Four deliberately faulty local variants are rejected by the oracles: wrapping
multiplication, wrapping addition, admitting PB from capacity alone, and omitting
primitive undo. These mutation sources and logs remain ignored local artifacts.

Measured cost
-------------
Sequential B/C/C/B JVMs compare the same original models to 6795d7a3, with five
warmup iterations and seven samples per process (14 samples per revision).
Each model receives the same 262,144-work slice, 8M request cap and 128 MiB
reservation cap. No other regression process was run during these measurements.
All work totals and witness/status signatures match between revisions.

  Engine  Models  Median slice-batch time       Thread cumulative allocation
  LCG        45    608.4402 -> 546.2370 ms        879,626,020 -> 588,919,276 bytes
  PB         86    294.7385 -> 288.9286 ms        525,332,764 -> 468,175,496 bytes

LCG batch time is 10.22% lower and allocation 33.05% lower. PB allocation is
10.88% lower; its 1.97% timing difference is small and should not be treated as
a reliable whole-planner acceleration. Cumulative allocation is neither retained
heap nor RSS. These slice measurements do not establish a new solved case.

Complete regression
-------------------
At the same 20M cap, all 822 count-model/order records exactly match 6795d7a3
in result and work: 787 conclusions, 17 UNKNOWN and 18 unsupported conversions,
with 950,980,421 total recorded work. The 72 parity-neighbor records likewise
match, including work. The five original-order UNKNOWN cases therefore remain.
The 717 curated and 2,400 expanded graph fixture/order records also retain
their complete results and work; this is not only a no-lost-witness check.

All 73 standard programs pass. All 57 captured-stock requests verify across
one worker, four workers at the same cap, and four workers with the explicit
production 2x allowance. Offline assembly succeeds and all 202 production Java
sources in the source JAR match the working tree byte-for-byte. Captured-network
replay is not a new live host execution test. Input/source hashes, sample ranges,
negative controls and campaign settings are recorded in
tests/provenance/exact-row-activities-20261011.json.

Remaining tool experiment
-------------------------
Adding the retained decision queue to the isolated finite-support/CDCL prototype
keeps its 17/32 sparse-equality conclusions at the same 20M direct-entry allowance.
An exact domain-fixpoint cache then reaches 18/32 without losing those 17 answers,
but still resolves none of the four large sparse residuals. Its one additional
answer is already solved by the production portfolio. The experiment remains
outside production; it is not evidence of a new main-stage solution, and lacks
the complete resource/proof integration needed for admission.

Broader numeric/closure admission and the audit's separate host-residency and
end-to-end attribution gaps (H/J) remain open. This boundary does not claim
general SCIP/CP-SAT equivalence or a new main-stage solved hard case.
