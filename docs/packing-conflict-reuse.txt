Scoped conflict reuse when packing enumeration resumes

The packing portfolio already retains its exact enumeration while a private
Boolean conflict-learning repair searches the same face. Previously, an
inconclusive repair discarded its clauses before resuming that enumeration.
The preserved DFS stack therefore could not use implications learned during
the repair. This change carries a bounded selection into that same stack.

Identity is the scope contract: the two neighborhoods must share the original
coefficient array, bound array and budget. These arrays include the column
order, proposed fixed exterior and any distance restriction. A different
projection, even one with the same dimensions or equal entries, is declined.
Nothing is published as a whole-model exclusion, and no full search allowance
or phase deadline is renewed. The existing original-model verifier remains
responsible for returned candidates.

The snapshot includes live learned clauses from the repair's current database
and binary implications from its watch lists. Reading only the first 128
externally reported conflicts misses later useful clauses; reading only the
clause database misses binaries stored directly in the watches. Selection
prefers short clauses and then low decision-level counts/recent use. At most
64 conjunctions are retained, with an 8192-work selection scan plus bounded
domain checks and extraction. Only literal zero/one domains are accepted.
Offset integer domains cannot silently become Boolean bit coordinates.

The receiver reserves its bounded selection scratch, incidence arrays and
per-depth state before importing. It compares the clauses with the remaining
DFS subtrees, including unvisited siblings. Clauses incompatible with every
remaining subtree are discarded. A wholly irrelevant or refused snapshot
leaves the original enumeration in place without a clause-checking workspace.

Two incidence masks per variable remove all clauses contradicted by a chosen
value together. Each DFS level has its own remaining-clause mask. Propagation
checks only those clauses: a completed forbidden conjunction closes the local
branch, while one remaining literal forces its opposite. Those implications
feed the existing exact row activities. Backtracking selects the saved parent
state; it neither replays nor reallocates clauses. No temporary collections are
allocated per search node. The original suffix index and exact DFS choices are
retained, and source disposal cannot invalidate the copied masks.

CountPackingConflictTest independently enumerates the original Boolean models
to check every exported conjunction and the resumed solver's status/witness.
It also covers high bit 63, sibling restoration, offset-domain refusal, the
bounded snapshot scan, binary-watch storage, existing DFS states, irrelevant
remaining subtrees, strict projection identity, worker movement, memory refusal,
repeated import, cancellation and work exhaustion. Deliberate faults exercise
polarity, binary omission, scope leakage, sibling state, implication disposal
and the residual-subtree admission check.

This is communication between existing complementary searches, not a new LP
basis algorithm or a completeness claim for multidimensional packing. Local
conflict reuse need not solve a canonical hard input to be useful; its full
family results and measured costs must remain explicit.

Validation at 0.1.6 against 27672879, unchanged 40M work

All 296 canonical packing inputs preserve their previous outcomes: 146
verified positives and 150 unresolved. Of the old positives, 145 have exactly
the same reported work; mknapcb6_09_bks rises from 36,120,189 to 36,124,022.
Their total rises from 1,077,498,255 to 1,077,502,088. No canonical hard case
is newly solved. Thirty retained final diagnostic traces contain imported
conflicts/implications; those counts may revisit the same consequence and
are not distinct solutions or an explanation of every remaining limit.

Twenty-two targeted programs pass. The new exhaustive check includes 256
retained continuations, 113 nonempty snapshots and 232 conjunctions checked
against every original Boolean witness. It checks the real owner's handoff
as well as the standalone receiver. Seven deliberate faults are detected.
Offline assembly, 1478 ABI entries, source/JAR correspondence and Forge
packaging with embedded AE dump pass.
Three historical inputs (the affected passing control, a native-solver
positive and an unresolved control) also retain every outcome under recipe
orders 0, 1, 17 and 97, both serially and with four workers at the same cap.
That paired comparison executes 48 requests; it adds no canonical recovery.

A balanced A/B/B/A kernel comparison uses 16 warmups and 12 measured rounds
per process, without other CGSE tests running. Its 400,000-step samples keep
the exact frontier hash, 592,834 work and 1216-byte peak reserved index storage.
Median wall time is 10.8017 -> 11.0210 ms; quantized caller CPU medians are both
15.625 ms. Cumulative thread allocation is 16,592 -> 16,624 bytes (four search
objects), with no per-node clause allocation. This small isolated measurement
does not establish whole-request or live-server latency, heap size or RSS.

The canonical ledger remains 150 packing + 4 sparse equalities + 2 source/fuel
allocations. This increment does not rerun the entire historical corpus,
the other hard families, native solvers or the live host.
